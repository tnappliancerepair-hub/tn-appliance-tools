The Technician Decision Report

Four options, real pricing, your call

Every repair starts with a real technician reviewing your model and symptoms. You always see the whole picture and choose — no pressure, no mystery "all-in" number with the markup buried inside.

Option 1
OEM Part Only
We source the exact OEM part and ship it to you. You install. Best for confident DIYers who want guaranteed fit.
Option 2
Budget Part Only
A verified compatible part at a lower price, shipped to your door. You install. Great when the fit is straightforward.
Option 3
OEM Part + We Install
We source the OEM part and our technician installs it. Best when fit is critical or access is tricky.
Option 4
Budget Part + We Install
We source an equivalent part and install it — the balance of cost and convenience.
