C

The three things that will bite you

If everything else slips a little, protect these. In order of how much it hurts.

1. A completed warranty job that never gets submitted

= money we did the work for and never collect. This is the single thing to master with Danielle. Every finished warranty job → filed with the vendor. Warranty Review shows you the day's completions with the package ready.

2. A job that never gets scheduled

= a lost customer, often a warranty one who quietly falls off. The Needs Scheduled queue is your inbox — don't let it grow. If you can't schedule a job because you lack the customer's availability, that's what the availability chase is for.

3. A parts return that misses its deadline

= we eat the part cost (a core charge), and sometimes lose the claim. Every return label has a clock. Log it, make sure it ships, confirm it went back.

D

Your safety net

You're not doing this from memory. Three backstops.

  • 🐜 Ask Ant — the blue button on every office page. “What needs my attention right now?” · “Who should I put on job 20114?” · “Reschedule job 20114 to Friday.” It previews and asks you to confirm before it changes anything.
  • The written runbook — docs/danielle-runbook.md has the button-level “how do I…” for every common scenario (new warranty email, reschedule request, tech running late, something looks broken).
  • The watchers keep running — customer confirmations, tech assignment texts, no-show checks, the missed-call callback net. You are the escalation point now, so the alerts come to you — but the automation still does the repetitive work.
