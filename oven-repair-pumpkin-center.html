Common Oven problems we see in Pumpkin Center, LA

Around Pumpkin Center and the Tangipahoa communities just outside Hammond, here are the oven or range calls that come in most — and what they usually turn out to be:

  • Won't heat / weak bake — a failed bake element on electric, or a worn igniter on gas — the single most common oven repair there is.
  • Bakes but won't broil (or the reverse) — just the corresponding element or igniter — not the whole oven.
  • Won't hold temperature / burns food — a failed oven temperature sensor or a thermostat that needs recalibrating.
  • Gas burners won't light — clogged igniters or a failed spark module.
  • Electric surface element won't heat — the element itself, the receptacle it plugs into, or the infinite switch.
  • Dead panel or an F-code — the control board or a tripped safety — the code usually points right at the cause.
