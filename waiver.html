• Old water lines, hoses, valves, shut-offs, or flooring that have to be disturbed when a machine is pulled out and moved to work on it. Aged connections and floors can wear, leak, or fail when a unit that's sat for years is moved — even when it's handled carefully.

By signing below, you understand that TN Appliance Exchange isn't responsible for problems caused by those hidden or pre-existing conditions, or for old water lines, connections, hoses, or flooring that are disturbed by the normal, careful moving of your appliance.
