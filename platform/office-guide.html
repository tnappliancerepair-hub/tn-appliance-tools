Danielle — your day on the new board

This is a test copy of the office board loaded with a normal day of jobs. Nothing here is a real customer. Poke at everything, do the things you'd do on a real day, and jot down anything that feels harder than the old way — that list is what we fix next.

Signing in — open tnapplianceexchange.net/platform/office-board.html and enter the email + password Teddy gives you. Same login works for the scheduling calendar and messages.

1 See your whole day

office-board.html

Every job is a card in a column by where it's at: New · Scheduled · In progress · Waiting on parts · Completed. The little number on each column button is the count. The money bar up top shows collected, outstanding, and tax for the month.

💬Right now you find a job by tapping a column to filter, then scrolling — there's no search box yet. If you catch yourself wanting to type a name to jump to it, tell us — that's an easy add.

2 Schedule a job

Dispatch (top of the board → 📅 Dispatch)

  1. The Unscheduled tray lists every job with no day/tech.
  2. Tap one — it suggests the best tech + day (who covers that ZIP, who has room, who's already routed nearby).
  3. Tap Use this, or pick your own tech + day, then Save.

It lands on the board as Scheduled and texts the tech. You can also drag/assign right from a job card.

3 Open a job — assign, text, notes

office-board.html → tap any card

The drawer is the job's cockpit: assign or change the tech, flag 2-man / long job, add office notes, and text the customer right there — the whole conversation is in the drawer (their texts and yours, plus any photos/video they sent). Their stated availability shows under intake.

4 Build the invoice & take payment

job drawer → invoice worksheet

  1. Enter labor, parts (type your cost — the customer price fills in from your margin), trip fee, tax. Totals add up live.
  2. Save, then Text invoice or Print.
  3. On the payment card: Take card (texts a pay link) or Mark cash / check.

5 Warranty jobs

Warranty jobs carry the company (AHS, SquareTrade…) and claim #. The drawer holds everything you file with: the customer's video, the model-sticker photo, the diagnosis, and the parts.

💬You still file the claim by hand in the vendor's portal (same as today) by copying those fields over. The old board gave you one-tap "copy this field" cards and a parts-owed-back list — those aren't here yet. As you file a couple, tell us how much you miss them.

When the warranty check comes in: tap Remittance on the board and split the EFT/check back across the jobs it paid — set what actually came in per job and mark them paid.

6 Parts you're waiting on

A job waiting on a part sits in the Waiting on parts column, and the part shows in the drawer.

💬There's no separate "to-order" list yet (with a required ETA + auto-text to the customer) like the old one. If you rely on that to keep parts moving, flag it.

7 Settings

office-board.html → Settings

Business info, sales tax, ways to pay, the review link, and your own password — all in one place.

✍️ Before you close it — jot this down

One or two lines each is plenty. Be blunt — "this was slower," "I couldn't find X," "the old way was better because…" This is exactly what we build next.

  • Scheduling — could you get a job onto a tech's day as fast as today?
  • Warranty — filing a claim + tracking the money and parts-owed-back: what slowed you down?
  • Parts — could you keep track of what's on order and chase it?
  • Invoicing & money — build it, send it, mark it paid: anything clunky?
  • Texting customers — was the conversation easy to find and use?
  • Trusting the board — did you ever worry a job was missing or dropped? (The old "everything's accounted for" banner isn't here yet — did you miss it?)
