Straight answers

The questions people actually ask.

Do I have to cancel my current software first?

No, and we'd tell you not to. Run both. The import is read-only — we never write to or change your current system, so there's nothing to undo if you decide to stay.

What if the import misses something?

You see a preview of exactly what we found before anything lands, and you can run it again. It's built to be repeatable — a second run updates what's there instead of creating duplicates.

Which systems can you import from?

Housecall Pro is live and proven against tens of thousands of real jobs. Jobber and Workiz are built and get their tuning pass with the first shop that brings one. On anything else, talk to us — most systems can export, and we'd rather build the adapter than lose you over it.

How long until my phone is answering?

Calls, about ten minutes — we get you a number and the AI is live on it. Texting takes 1–3 business days because US carriers require every business to be registered before it can text customers. We file that paperwork for you; you just give us your EIN.

What does it cost when I add a tech?

Nothing. That's the whole point of a flat shop price. Hire your fifth tech and your bill doesn't move.

Can I get my data back out?

Yes. It's your data. We'd be a bad bet if the only thing keeping you here was that leaving was hard — that's the trap you're trying to get out of right now.
