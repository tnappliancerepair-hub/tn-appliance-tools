Common LG Issues

What We See Most Often

LG refrigerators have known failure patterns our techs have seen across hundreds of jobs. Knowing the platform's quirks means faster diagnosis and the right part the first time.

💧 Leaking water / ice on the bottom of the freezer

Water on the floor, water pooling inside the fridge, or a sheet of ice under the bottom drawers is almost always a clogged, frozen defrost drain — it backs up, refreezes, and overflows. It's the most common fridge repair there is, and an easy, affordable fix: we clear and thaw the drain and add a drain heater/clip so it doesn't re-clog. Common on Samsung, Frigidaire and GE side-by-sides, and virtually all top-freezers.

🧊 Linear compressor (our specialty)

When an LG stops cooling entirely and the freezer warms up, it's usually the linear compressor. We do these all the time — we have the recovery, brazing, and vacuum tools and the technicians trained on the sealed system, so we replace it right the first time. Many LG models carry a limited warranty of up to 10 years on the compressor part; if yours qualifies, you mostly pay labor — and our labor beats the shops that treat a compressor like a whole new fridge.

❄️ Ice maker (our specialty)

No ice, slow ice, or a jammed dispenser on French door and door-in-door LGs — the ice maker assembly, water inlet valve, or a frozen line. We stock and source these constantly, so it's a fast, affordable fix, not a week-long wait. This is our other bread-and-butter LG repair.

Evaporator fan & defrost

A frosted-over evaporator or a dead evap fan warms the fridge while the freezer still looks fine — often mistaken for a compressor. A quick, affordable fix once we confirm it, which is exactly why the diagnosis matters.

Water inlet valve & dispenser

Weak or no water at the dispenser, or an ice tray that won't fill, usually points to the inlet valve or a frozen dispenser line — not the whole cooling system.

Main control & sensors

Wrong temps, error codes, or a dead display can be the main control board or a bad thermistor. We confirm it's actually the board before replacing it.

FAQ

LG Customers Ask

Is my LG refrigerator covered under the class action?
Possibly. Specific serial number ranges of LG linear compressor refrigerators (2014–2017 manufacture, certain models) are covered. We check serial during the diagnostic.
How long do LG refrigerators last?
8–12 years typical. Linear compressor reliability is the dominant question. The latest generation is improved over earlier versions.
Is it worth fixing an LG refrigerator?
Most non-sealed-system repairs yes. Linear compressor failure on a 5+ year unit is the gray-area conversation where replacement often wins.
What is the LG linear compressor problem?
Some early LG linear compressors had a manufacturing issue causing premature failure. Affected units lose cooling entirely. LG covered the parts under a class settlement; labor is separately negotiable. We check status during the diagnostic.
Do I have to pay the diagnostic fee AND the repair cost?
No. Diagnostic fees apply directly to repair labor. You pay once.
