Community Partners — honest appliance help for the people you serve

When an appliance breaks, our $50 Quick Check gives the people your organization serves an honest answer from a real technician — the exact part shipped to their door, credited toward the repair. Free for your organization.
A working fridge, a working stove, a working washer — for a family on a tight budget, that's not a luxury, it's the difference between a hard week and a good one. We named the company Ant after our founder's son, Anthony. This is Anthony's Gift — our way of helping the people you already fight for. 🐜

"Truly I tell you, whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me." — Matthew 25:40

Who this is for

If your organization serves families who could use an honest, affordable hand when an appliance breaks, this program is for you — free, no paperwork:

🥫 Food banks & pantries 🧓 Senior centers 🌎 Immigrant & refugee groups 🏠 Shelters & transitional housing 🏢 Apartment communities 🎓 Schools & PTAs ❤️ Nonprofits & charities ⛪ Churches

What the people you serve get

An honest $50 Quick Check — no $125–150 service-call surprise. A real technician reviews their video, tells them the exact part they need, and ships it to their door — or walks them through fixing it themselves. The $50 is credited toward the repair, and it works anywhere in the U.S.
