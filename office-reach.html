📲 Reach Me

When a caller asks Ant for a person, should it ring your phone — or just take a message?

Live calls to a human are
…
• On — Ant transfers callers who ask for a person to your cell. They stay on the business line, so your number stays private.
• Off — Ant takes a message instead (your phone stays quiet). Flip this off when you're on vacation.
• Either way, Ant still answers + handles everything else.
