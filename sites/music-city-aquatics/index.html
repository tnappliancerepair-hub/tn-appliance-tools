Questions

People also ask

Do you service saltwater and reef tanks?
Yes — fresh, salt, and reef. Reef is where the expertise really matters, and it's what we live for. We handle water chemistry, livestock health, equipment, and the dosing that keeps a reef thriving.
Do you offer recurring maintenance?
Absolutely — weekly, biweekly, or monthly on a set route. Water changes, testing, cleaning, dosing, and equipment checks, and we log your water every visit so problems get caught early. You never have to think about your tank.
What if my tank is crashing after hours?
Call our after-hours line at (615) 243-8595. A crashing reef is thousands of dollars of coral and fish on the line — we'd rather help you save it than have you wait until morning.
Can you set up a brand-new aquarium?
Yes. We design and build custom tanks — freshwater, saltwater, and full reef systems — from equipment and plumbing to aquascaping and stocking, then keep it healthy on a plan.
Where are you located and what areas do you serve?
Our shop is at 4963 Lebanon Pike, Old Hickory, TN 37138 — come browse freshwater and saltwater fish, corals, and live plants. We also deliver and service tanks across Greater Nashville: Hermitage, Donelson, Mt. Juliet, Hendersonville, Gallatin, Lebanon, Brentwood and the surrounding communities.
