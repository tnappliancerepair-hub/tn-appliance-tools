Questions dealers ask

FAQ

We sell appliances but don't have a service department — can you be it?
Yes — that's exactly what this is. When a customer needs a repair, an install fix, or a warranty call, you hand it to us and we handle it fast, in your customer's home, so you keep the sale and the relationship.
Will you try to take our customers?
No. We work behind the scenes as your service partner — there to make you look good and keep your customer happy, not to poach them. That's the whole basis of the partnership.
Can you handle delivery and install issues, not just repairs?
Yes. Dented-in-transit, won't-level, water-line hookup, a unit that arrived not working — we back up your delivery and install so a problem at the door doesn't turn into a return.
How does pricing work?
Partner pricing with one point of contact. We can bill you or the customer depending on how you run it, and we give you honest repair-vs-replace calls so you're never guessing on a warranty or goodwill fix.
What brands do you service?
All major brands — Whirlpool, GE, Samsung, LG, Maytag, Frigidaire, Kenmore, Bosch, KitchenAid, Electrolux, Amana and more — across refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ranges and microwaves.
