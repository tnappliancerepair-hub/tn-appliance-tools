Questions property managers ask

FAQ

Do you coordinate directly with our tenants?
Yes. Send us the unit and tenant contact (or forward the tenant to us) and we text them directly to confirm the issue, capture a photo or video, and set up access. You never have to relay messages back and forth.
How do you bill us?
One consolidated monthly statement across all your properties, on net terms. Every work order is itemized by property and unit, and you can download invoices from your portal any time.
Can we see the status of every work order?
Yes — a portfolio portal shows every job across every property in real time (scheduled, in progress, waiting on parts, completed) with photos and the technician's notes.
Do you help decide repair vs. replace?
On every job. You own the appliance, so we give you an honest repair-vs-replace call with real cost numbers — we don't upsell repairs that aren't worth it on aging units.
What brands do you service?
All major brands — Whirlpool, GE, Samsung, LG, Maytag, Frigidaire, Kenmore, Bosch, KitchenAid, Electrolux, Amana and more, across refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ranges and microwaves.
