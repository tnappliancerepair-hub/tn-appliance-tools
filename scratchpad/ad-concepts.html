How these get made

The production path

  • Static ads (Boulders, Message-vs-Job, 4 Options) — designed here; final adds a hero render/screenshot. Runnable on Meta the day the account's connected.
  • Cinematic spots (Founder Film, Broken at 2am) — full script + scene-by-scene AI-generation prompts + voiceover, in the production kit. You (or an editor) render; the finished clip drops into the live one-tap fan-out to FB/IG/TikTok/YouTube.
  • Distribution is already live — caption, clip, and post-everywhere all built. We're only adding the creative.

What unlocks each channel (your call — no spend happens without you)

  • AI voiceover — vault ELEVENLABS_API_KEY.
  • Meta ads — vault META_AD_ACCOUNT_ID + META_ADS_TOKEN, then flip ACTIVE.
  • ChatGPT ads — ready now; flip a campaign live.
  • Google ads — consumer already running; SaaS staged.
  • Set the creator commission % for the influencer program.
