04When consent is collected

Consent is requested AFTER the customer has described their issue and selected a service tier, but BEFORE any phone number is collected. The phone number input field is hidden until the customer makes an affirmative choice on the consent card.

Sequence guarantee: the chat text input is programmatically disabled while the consent card is showing. The customer cannot bypass it by typing — the only way forward is to click one of the two buttons.

06How to opt out

  • Reply STOP to any message from us to immediately unsubscribe.
  • You will receive one final confirmation message confirming you have been unsubscribed.
  • Reply START to resubscribe at any time.
  • Other recognized opt-out keywords: CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, REVOKE, STOPALL, OPTOUT.
