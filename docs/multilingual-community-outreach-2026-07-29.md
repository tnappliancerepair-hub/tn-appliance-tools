## The strategy in one line
The pages are built and the demand is real (**~113k+ in-language searches/mo, almost no competition**). Now we make the communities *know*, three ways, in order of power:
1. **In-language reviews** (now automated — a happy customer is asked to review *in their language* so their community finds us). ← biggest trust lever
2. **Be where they already look** — post our OWN in-language listing on the community boards + Facebook groups (do NOT link to those boards from our site; just be present on them).
3. **Community partnerships** — extend **Anthony's Gift** ($25 off) to ethnic churches, cultural centers, and grocers, exactly like the church program. Genuine relationships → word-of-mouth + real backlinks.

**Rule: never link OUT to the Craigslist-style ethnic boards** (rusrek, KinhDoanhUSA, etc.) — it hands them our Google authority and sends our visitor to a worse option. Instead, POST our own listing there.

## The tracking link (gives the $25 discount + tracks the source + renders in their language)
```
tnapplianceexchange.net/quick-check-intake.html?lang={CODE}&partner={ORG-CODE}
```
- `{CODE}` = `es` · `ru` · `vi` · `fr` · `ar` · `zh` · `hi`
- `{ORG-CODE}` = a short code you make up per org, e.g. `MQVN-NOLA`, `SLAVIC-NASH`, `PLAZA-MARIACHI`
- Example: `…/quick-check-intake.html?lang=vi&partner=MQVN-NOLA` → Vietnamese page, $25 Anthony's Gift, tracked to Mary Queen of Vietnam.
- The $25 comes off automatically (Anthony's Gift `partner` code). Kill switch if ever overwhelmed: vault `COMMUNITY_GIFT_PAUSED=true`.

## How to run it (weekly, ~30 min)
1. **Reviews** run themselves now — every happy in-language customer is asked to review in their language. Just keep doing great work.
2. **Post one listing** per week on a community board or FB group (rotate languages). Make a unique `partner` code each time so you can see what works.
3. **Reach one org** per week with the note above + their own tracking link. Churches and groceries are the highest-trust multipliers.
4. **Watch what converts** — the `partner` codes show which community/org sends real jobs; lean into those.
