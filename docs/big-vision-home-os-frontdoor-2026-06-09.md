## Appliance isn't the market. It's the wedge.

Strip Ant to what it *is*: a system that answers calls, schedules, dispatches,
captures the work report, submits warranty, chases parts, and keeps customers
informed — **with no human in the seat.** The word "appliance" isn't in that
sentence. It's a **home-services operating system.** Appliance is just where
it's being proven.

Every home-services trade has the identical pain (small shops, 2–10 techs,
drowning dispatcher, warranty paperwork, parts hunting, repeat-visit waste):
HVAC, plumbing, electrical, garage doors, pool/spa, pest, locksmith, handyman.

**TAM proof:** ServiceTitan built a multi-billion-$ company on HVAC/plumbing/
electrical software. But they're expensive, complex, enterprise-y, NOT
AI-native, and NOT operator-built — they priced out and ignored the small shops.
**Ant's wedge vs them: AI-native + operator-built + replaces the human (labor
line), aimed at the shops ServiceTitan won't serve, in the warranty lane they
don't even compete in.**

## Layer 1 — Your superpower picks the expansion order

Expand where your edge is sharpest. Your edge = **warranty** (AHS relationship,
clean claim capture, submission automation). So: **most warranty-heavy trades
first.**
- Appliance (you) — warranty-heavy ✅
- **HVAC (#2)** — extremely warranty-heavy, bigger tickets, manufacturer rebates ✅✅
- Plumbing / electrical — less warranty, more cash/insurance, ServiceTitan's
  strongest turf → later or never.

## Layer 2 — Each trade is cheaper than the last

~80% shared, 20% swappable. The **OS layer** (calls, scheduling, dispatch,
comms, office automation, warranty-submission engine, The Plug) is identical
across trades. Only the **domain brain** (diagnosis, parts, failure modes)
swaps — and it builds itself via the data flywheel + a trade-specific Plug.
Expansion **accelerates**; the platform compounds while competitors rebuild from
scratch each time.

## Layer 3 — The two vectors converge at the home

- **Horizontal:** more trades (appliance → HVAC → …)
- **Vertical:** deeper into the same home (the consumer/homeowner play)

**Same destination.** Touch multiple trades in one house → accumulate a **"home
systems record"** (every appliance, unit, repair, part, failure, per address).
Nobody has that. It's the foundation of the consumer platform AND a moat no
single-trade competitor can assemble. Adjacent-trades + homeowner dream =
**the operating system for the home.**
