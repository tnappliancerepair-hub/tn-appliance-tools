# New Tenant Onboarding Guide

If you just signed up for Ant via `tnapplianceexchange.net/signup.html`, here's your day-1 checklist.

## What you got

A welcome SMS with your admin link. Tap it. That opens **company-admin.html** where you'll spend the first 30 minutes.

## Day 1 setup (30 min)

1. **Verify your shop info.** Open company-admin → confirm name, owner phone, owner email are right.
2. **Add your techs.** From the hub or directly at `tech-onboard.html?company_id=YOUR_ID`. Each tech needs first name, last name, phone (E.164), commission rate (default 40%).
3. **Send each tech their PIN.** PIN setup is currently manual; we'll text you a temporary PIN for each tech to share.
4. **Set up Telnyx FROM numbers (optional).** If you want SMS to come from your own number, get a Telnyx number, then update company.telnyx_from_customer + telnyx_from_tech in company-admin.
5. **Set timezone.** America/Chicago is default; change if needed.

## Day 2-7 (using the system)

- **Office:** open `office.html` as your home base. Calendar / Todo / Pulse / Customer Search.
- **Techs:** install `tech-daily-dashboard.html` as a home-screen icon (PWA). Tap into a job → tech-ant-live opens with Ant chat.
- **Customers:** they get auto-text on every booking with a link to their customer-portal.

## Day 8-30 (your trial)

You have full feature access. No gates. We watch your usage and check in if you get stuck.

## After day 30

Stripe Checkout subscription kicks in. $49/active tech/month. Billed monthly. Cancel from company-admin anytime.

## Need help?

Text the same number we welcomed you with. We answer fast.

🐜 Long Live Ant.
