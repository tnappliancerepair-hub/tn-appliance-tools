# After-Hours Aggression — Ad Plan (2026-08-20)

## The thesis (one line)
**We're the only appliance shop in our markets that can SERVE a lead at 2am** (AI chat
that books the job + Ann answering the phone 24/7). So we buy attention *when
competitors are dark* — cheaper clicks, uncontested auction, peak-intent customer — and
our automated funnel converts it while they sleep.

## Why this is a moat, not a gimmick
1. **We're the only one who responds.** After hours a customer finds 3 shops; two go to
   voicemail, one (us) books the job. The lead isn't just cheaper — it's the only one that
   gets served.
2. **The auction is cheaper when they're closed.** Fewer advertisers bidding evenings /
   nights / weekends → lower CPC, more share of voice for less money, exactly when we're
   strongest and they're weakest.
3. **After-hours failures are peak-intent.** Fridge warm in the morning, dryer dead during
   evening laundry — anxious buyer who wants it handled NOW. Win them before they shop at 9am.

## Keywords — high-intent repair, not browse
- Core: `appliance repair near me`, `[appliance] repair near me`, `[appliance] repair [city]`,
  `same day appliance repair`.

- Negatives (kill the wrong intent): `parts`, `used`, `for sale`, `DIY`, `manual`,
  `how to`, `job`, `salary` — plus the used-store ghost terms.

## What "winning" looks like

  cost per booked job** on the after-hours windows (cheaper night clicks should beat daytime).
- After-hours windows converting **at or above** the daytime rate at **lower CPC** = thesis
  proven → scale hard into those hours.
