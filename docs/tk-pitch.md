## Delivery notes (so it never sounds salesy)
- **Say it slower than feels natural, and let it breathe.** The confidence is in *not* pushing.
- **Kill every hype word** — no "revolutionary," "game-changer," "best." Just true and plain.
- **Lean on the proof no competitor has:** *"I run my whole shop on it every single day."*
- **End on the offer, then stop talking.** "That's the whole deal." Let the silence work.
- **It's an offer, not a close.** You're handing over the keys, not asking for a yes.
