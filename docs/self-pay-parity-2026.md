# 🎯 STRATEGY — Self-pay to warranty-parity by end of 2026 (via automation)

Saved 2026-06-13 (Teddy). The positioning that ties the whole build together.

## The reality + the goal
- **Warranty has been ~95% of our work** for years. We keep doing it as well as we
  possibly can — that's the base.
- **We want self-pay jobs to grow** — and by **end of 2026, self-pay jobs should be
  as good as warranty jobs, if not better.**
- The lever that makes that possible is **automation**: Ant runs the friction-y
  parts so every job — warranty or self-pay — is faster, cleaner, and conflict-free.

## How automation gets us there
- **Ant handles the calls** (Vapi inbound) — booking, status, questions, 24/7.
- **Ant handles scheduling** — auto-schedule + the office one-screen flow.
- **Ant handles parts ordering** (and, with Marcone/Tribles APIs, real-time ship).
- **Changes no longer come with conflict.** When a tech or customer changes
  something (reschedule, parts, scope), it goes **through the Ant system** — one
  source of truth, everyone sees the same job. No phone-tag, no double-entry, no
  "who said what."
- **Even warranty jobs get better:** pre-diagnosed before the truck rolls → fixed
  faster (first-visit-fix) → TDR + warranty package processed faster + more
  efficiently. Automation lifts the 95%, not just the new self-pay slice.
- **Self-pay customers get the extras** that make the experience premium: new
  hoses installed, dryer clean-outs, leak detectors, coil cleans, etc. — all
  offered **simply through the portal, text, or Vapi.** (Add-on engine — built.)

## Why self-pay can become BETTER than warranty
Warranty is gated by the vendor (auth, approved parts, their pace). Self-pay we
control end-to-end: instant booking, honest 4-option TDR, the customer picks,
auto-schedule, parts on the way, add-ons, tips, online payment. With automation
doing the legwork, the self-pay experience can be faster + more transparent than
anything a warranty vendor allows.

## What's already built toward this (a lot)
- Ant inbound calls (Vapi, unified assistant) · auto-schedule + needs-scheduled
  flow · the single-source-of-truth job spine (write-once, every surface reads it)
  · pre-diagnosis push to Teddy + tech · TDR system + 4-option model · add-on
  engine (portal/text/tech) · online payments + tips + tax · parts ledger ·
  Danielle + techs texting/talking to Ant · morning briefings.

## What moves the needle next (after HCP cutover + daily adoption)
1. **Self-checkout $50 quick-check → TDR → 4 options → auto-schedule → auto-ship**
   (the North Star — `docs/self-checkout-vision.md`). This IS the self-pay engine.
2. **Conflict-free changes everywhere** — make every reschedule/parts/scope change
   (tech, customer, office) flow through Ant with the job as the single truth.
3. **Parts real-time** (Marcone/Tribles APIs) — auto-order + ship + ETA.
4. **First-visit-fix metric** — track it; it's the proof automation is working for
   both warranty and self-pay.

## The measure of success (EOY 2026)
A self-pay customer's experience — speed, clarity, options, communication — is
**at least as good as** a warranty job, and self-pay volume is climbing because of
it. Warranty stays excellent; automation lifts everything.
