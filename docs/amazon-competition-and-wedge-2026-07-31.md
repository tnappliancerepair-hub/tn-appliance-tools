# Amazon appliance-parts store — competition + how we compete (2026-07-31)

**Q (Teddy):** who's the competition on Amazon and how do we win the market?
**Companion to** `amazon-store-strategy-2026-07-18.md` + `amazon-parts-store-tiers-2026-07-31.md`.

## The competition — 4 clusters
1. **OEM brands** (Whirlpool, GE, Samsung, LG, Maytag/EveryDrop). Genuine, trusted, premium.
   → We match genuine via Marcone at a better price + undercut with a value tier they lack.
2. **Big-catalog OEM retailers** — **RepairClinic** (4M+ parts, same-day, price-match),
   **PartSelect** (2M+ OEM, model diagrams/videos), AppliancePartsPros, Encompass.
   Moat = breadth + fitment data. Weakness = faceless, OEM-only, strongest on their OWN sites.
3. **Amazon aftermarket flippers** — Edgewater Parts, AMI Parts, Beaquicy, Waterdrop (filters),
   + long tail (Podoy, Techecook, Rongoble…). Volume competition for the value tier.
   Weakness = anonymous, no expertise, sloppy fitment → high returns, thin listings, no support.
4. **Reman / renewed** — CoreCentric's PartSimple + scattered sellers. Fragmented; nobody owns it.

## The wedge (how we win — specific)
We do NOT out-catalog RepairClinic or out-price a flipper on one SKU. We win as the one thing
none of them are: **the parts store run by real techs that sells every price tier of the same
part, confirms it fits, and helps you install it.**

1. **Tier ladder nobody offers** — open-box → reman → off-brand → OEM of the *same* part in one
   store. Flippers = aftermarket only; RepairClinic/PartSelect = OEM only. "Pick your budget."
2. **Fitment + AI confirm = returns killer** — wrong-part returns are the category's #1 pain AND
   Amazon's #1 rank-killer. QR "scan to confirm it fits / talk to Ant" + exhaustive fitment →
   lower returns → higher rank + Buy Box. Our structural edge (Ant Brain model→part corpus).
3. **Real named techs (E-E-A-T)** — TN Appliance, techs since 2012, 1,081 reviews, C-DET.
   A+ leads with "picked by working techs, not a warehouse." Uncopyable by flippers.
4. **External-traffic flywheel** — 500+ ranking pages (7 languages) + SMS/email base → drive
   outside traffic to listings → Amazon rewards rank + pays ~10% Brand Referral Bonus.

## Where NOT to fight
- Don't out-catalog RepairClinic day one. - Skip water filters as beachhead (Waterdrop owns it,
  saturated). - Don't race flippers to zero on a commodity SKU with no fitment edge.

## Beachhead
- **Reman control boards (CoreCentric)** — we have the cheap tier others lack; tech-confirm kills
  the "will a reman board work/fit" fear that suppresses the whole category.
- **Universal maintenance items** — SS braided hoses, dryer-vent kits, hard-start kits: no fitment
  risk, low returns, FBA-friendly, national → build review velocity + Buy Box, then expand.

## Positioning line
**"Every part, every budget — confirmed to fit, by real techs."**

## Execution phases
1. Beachhead SKUs (hoses/vent + a few hero reman boards): perfect listings, fitment lists,
   tech-authored A+, QR insert, priced across tiers.
2. Review flywheel: Vine + insert cards + our existing customer base; drive external traffic.
3. Expand model-specific catalog tier-by-tier; add commodity/filters once brand + reviews exist.
