# REFERRAL / RESELLER AGREEMENT

This Referral / Reseller Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of
**[EFFECTIVE DATE]** by and between **[COMPANY], a [STATE] [entity type]** ("**Company**"), which
owns and operates the AssistAnt software platform and related services (the "**Platform**"), and
**[PARTNER NAME / ENTITY]** ("**Partner**").

## 1. Purpose
Company wishes to appoint Partner to refer prospective customers ("**Referred Accounts**") to the
Platform, and Partner wishes to do so, on the terms below. The Parties' Mutual Non-Disclosure
Agreement dated **[NDA DATE]** is incorporated by reference and applies to all Confidential
Information exchanged under this Agreement.

## 2. Appointment
**2.1 Referral partner.** Company appoints Partner as a non-employee, independent referral
partner to introduce prospective customers to the Platform. Partner is an independent contractor;
nothing here creates a partnership, joint venture, agency, franchise, or employment relationship.

**2.2 Non-exclusive.** The appointment is **non-exclusive** [OPTIONAL: within the territory of
**[TERRITORY]**]. Company may market, sell, and appoint other partners anywhere, and Partner may
represent other products that do not compete with the Platform, subject to §7 (Confidentiality)
and §8 (Non-Circumvention).

**2.3 No authority to bind.** Partner has no authority to enter into contracts, make
commitments, set or quote prices, make warranties or representations, or otherwise act on
Company's behalf, and will not hold itself out as able to do so. Partner will not use Company's
name, marks, or the Platform's branding except as Company approves in writing.

## 3. Commission
**3.1 Rate.** For each Referred Account that becomes a paying subscriber to the Platform, Company
will pay Partner a commission of **[__]% of the net subscription revenue actually collected** from
that account **[OR: a flat $[__] per active account per month]**. "Net subscription revenue" means
subscription fees actually received by Company for the Platform, excluding taxes, third-party
pass-through charges (e.g. telephony/usage), refunds, chargebacks, and discounts.

**3.2 Duration.** Commission is payable **[for as long as the Referred Account remains an active
paying subscriber]** **[OR: for [__] months from the account's activation date]**. Commission
accrues only while the account is active and current on payment, and stops on cancellation,
non-payment, or downgrade to a non-paying status.

**3.3 Attribution.** An account is a "Referred Account" only if it is attributed to Partner in
Company's systems at signup (via Partner's referral code/link or by Company's onboarding record).
If two partners could claim the same account, Company's records control, and Company decides
attribution in good faith. Company's books and records are the basis for all commission
calculations, and Company will make a commission statement available to Partner.

**3.4 Payment.** Company will calculate commissions **monthly** and pay amounts due within
**[30] days** after the end of each month, by [method], provided Partner has furnished any
tax forms (e.g. IRS Form W-9) reasonably required. Company may offset or claw back commissions
paid on amounts later refunded, charged back, or uncollected.

**3.5 Expenses.** Partner bears its own costs of doing business. Partner is not entitled to any
compensation other than the commission in this §3.

## 4. Partner Obligations
Partner will: (a) represent the Platform honestly and only with materials Company approves;
(b) make no guarantees, performance claims, price quotes, or warranties beyond Company's
published materials; (c) comply with all applicable laws, including those governing marketing,
email/SMS, and privacy; (d) not make disparaging statements about Company or the Platform; and
(e) promptly pass Referred Account information to Company and let Company own the customer
relationship, contracting, onboarding, billing, and support.

## 5. Intellectual Property — **Company owns everything**
**5.1 Ownership.** As between the Parties, Company exclusively owns and retains all right, title,
and interest in and to the Platform and all of its software, source code, models, prompts,
workflows, data, designs, documentation, know-how, trademarks, and all other intellectual
property, including all improvements, modifications, and derivatives (collectively, "**Company
IP**"). This Agreement grants Partner **no** license or ownership in Company IP, and no right to
copy, modify, host, sublicense, resell, white-label, or create derivative works of the Platform,
except the limited right to refer prospects as described here.

**5.2 Feedback assignment.** If Partner provides any suggestions, ideas, feature requests, or
other feedback regarding the Platform ("**Feedback**"), Partner hereby irrevocably assigns to
Company all right, title, and interest in that Feedback, and Company may use it for any purpose
without restriction, attribution, or compensation. Partner acquires no ownership or other rights
in the Platform by contributing Feedback or by participating in its development or testing.

**5.3 No reverse engineering.** Partner will not, and will not permit others to, reverse
engineer, decompile, disassemble, scrape, or attempt to derive the source code or underlying
structure of the Platform, or build or assist in building a competing product using Company IP or
Confidential Information, except to the limited extent this restriction is prohibited by law.

## 6. Independent Contractor; Taxes
Partner is solely responsible for its own taxes, insurance, and business expenses, and for its
personnel. Nothing here entitles Partner to any employee benefits. Company will issue tax
information (e.g. IRS Form 1099) as required by law.

## 7. Confidentiality
The Parties' NDA governs all Confidential Information exchanged under this Agreement. If no NDA
is in effect, the confidentiality terms of that NDA are incorporated here by reference and apply.

## 8. Non-Circumvention; Non-Solicitation of Accounts
During the term and for **[12] months** after termination, Partner will not: (a) directly or
indirectly divert, solicit, or take any Referred Account away from Company; (b) refer, resell, or
steer any Referred Account (or a prospect first learned of through this relationship) to a product
that competes with the Platform; or (c) contract with a Referred Account to provide the same or
substantially similar services outside of Company. Company's ongoing relationship with each
Referred Account belongs to Company.

## 9. Term and Termination
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated. Either Party may
terminate for convenience on **[30] days'** written notice, or immediately for the other's
material breach that remains uncured **[10] days** after written notice. On termination:
(a) Partner stops representing the Platform and using Company's marks; (b) §§ 5, 7, 8, 10, and 11
survive; and (c) Company will pay commissions accrued on Referred Accounts through the
termination date, and will continue paying commissions on then-existing active Referred Accounts
for the remaining duration under §3.2, unless termination was for Partner's material breach or
breach of §5 or §8, in which case commissions cease on termination.

## 10. Warranties; Limitation of Liability; Indemnity
Each Party represents it has authority to enter this Agreement. THE PLATFORM IS PROVIDED TO
REFERRED ACCOUNTS UNDER COMPANY'S OWN CUSTOMER TERMS; PARTNER MAKES NO WARRANTIES ON COMPANY'S
BEHALF. EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF §5 (IP), §7 (CONFIDENTIALITY), OR §8 (NON-CIRCUMVENTION), NEITHER
PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, AND EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE
LIABILITY IS LIMITED TO THE COMMISSIONS PAID OR PAYABLE IN THE 12 MONTHS BEFORE THE CLAIM. Partner
will indemnify Company against claims arising from Partner's misrepresentations, unauthorized
statements, or violation of law.

## 11. Miscellaneous
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of **[GOVERNING-LAW STATE]** without regard to
conflict-of-laws principles. It, together with the NDA, is the entire agreement between the
Parties on its subject matter and supersedes prior discussions. It may be amended only in a
writing signed by both Parties. Neither Party may assign it without the other's written consent,
except to a successor of all or substantially all of its business. Notices must be in writing. If
any provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. This Agreement may be signed in
counterparts, including by electronic signature.
