# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of
**[EFFECTIVE DATE]** (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

- **[PLATFORM ENTITY], a [STATE] [entity type]** ("**Company**"), and
- **[PARTNER NAME / ENTITY]** ("**Partner**").

Company and Partner are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**." Each Party may
disclose Confidential Information to the other in connection with exploring and pursuing a
potential referral/reseller relationship regarding Company's software platform and related
services (the "**Purpose**").

## 1. Definition of Confidential Information
"**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the
"**Discloser**") to the other (the "**Recipient**"), in any form, that is designated as
confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature
or the circumstances of disclosure. It includes, without limitation: the Company platform and
its software, source code, architecture, data models, prompts, workflows, and internal tools;
business, product, and go-to-market strategy; pricing, margins, and unit economics; customer,
prospect, vendor, and partner lists and data; the terms of any potential arrangement between
the Parties; and any analyses, notes, or materials derived from the foregoing.

## 2. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can demonstrate:
(a) was publicly available at the time of disclosure or later became public through no fault of
the Recipient; (b) was already lawfully in the Recipient's possession without a duty of
confidentiality before disclosure; (c) was lawfully received from a third party without a duty
of confidentiality; or (d) was independently developed by the Recipient without use of or
reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 3. Obligations
The Recipient will: (a) use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; (b) protect it
with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less
than reasonable care; (c) not disclose it to any third party except to its employees, agents, or
advisors who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations at
least as protective as this Agreement; and (d) be responsible for any breach by such persons.

## 4. No Reverse Engineering; No Copying
The Recipient will not, and will not permit any third party to, reverse engineer, decompile,
disassemble, copy, reproduce, or attempt to derive the source code, structure, or underlying
ideas of any software, system, or tooling disclosed hereunder, except to the limited extent
this restriction is prohibited by applicable law.

## 5. No License; No Other Rights
Nothing in this Agreement grants the Recipient any license or right, by implication or
otherwise, to any Confidential Information or to any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret,
or other intellectual property of the Discloser, except the limited right to use the
Confidential Information for the Purpose. All Confidential Information remains the sole property
of the Discloser. **This Agreement conveys no ownership of and no right to use, resell, sublicense,
or build upon the Company platform** beyond the Purpose.

## 6. Return or Destruction
Upon the Discloser's written request or upon termination of the Parties' discussions, the
Recipient will promptly return or destroy (and, if requested, certify destruction of) all
Confidential Information and all copies, notes, and derivatives, except one archival copy that
may be retained solely for legal-compliance purposes and remains subject to this Agreement.

## 7. Term
This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either
Party on written notice. The confidentiality obligations survive for **[3] years** after such
termination; provided that obligations with respect to any information that constitutes a trade
secret continue for as long as such information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## 8. No Obligation; No Solicitation of Employees
This Agreement does not obligate either Party to proceed with any transaction or relationship.
During the term and for [12] months after, neither Party will directly solicit for employment
the other Party's employees whom it first learned of through disclosures under this Agreement
(general advertising not directed at such employees is not a breach).

## 9. Remedies
The Parties agree that unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information may cause
irreparable harm for which monetary damages are inadequate, and that the Discloser is entitled
to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies at law or equity, without the
requirement of posting a bond.

## 10. Miscellaneous
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of **[GOVERNING-LAW STATE]**, without regard
to conflict-of-laws principles. It is the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its
subject matter and supersedes prior discussions. It may be amended only in a writing signed by
both Parties. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect. Neither Party may
assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except to a successor of all or
substantially all of its business. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by
electronic signature.
