# 📹 How to verify your Google page (5–10 min, do it at home)

*Forward this to the tech. One per city: Lee = Clarksville · Andre = Hammond · John = Walker/Baton Rouge.*

**What this is:** Google needs a short video proving you really do appliance repair from your area before your
local Google page goes live. It's quick, it's honest — you're just showing your real setup.

---

## Before you start (2 min)
- Be **at home** (or wherever your van/tools live).
- On your phone, open the **Google Maps app** → tap your profile picture → **Your Business Profile** (Teddy
  added you as a manager, so it'll show "TN Appliance Exchange"). If it asks to verify, it'll say **"Record a
  video."**
- Have ready: your **van in the driveway**, your **tools**, and **a few appliance parts** (anything — a belt,
  a control board, an ice maker, a heating element).
- Good daylight. ~10 minutes.

## The video — ONE continuous take, ~1–2 minutes, no stopping or editing
Just walk and talk. Film these 4 things in order without cutting:

1. **Your work vehicle** — walk up to your van, show any logo/wrap + a bit of the street so it's clearly a
   real place you work from.
2. **Your tools + parts** — open the van (or garage), show your real gear and the appliance parts. This is the
   proof you actually do the work.
3. **You, on camera** — say it plain: *"I'm [name], I do appliance repair for TN Appliance Exchange here in
   [Clarksville / Hammond / Walker]."*
4. **The app** — turn the phone to show the **Business Profile** open on your screen, so Google sees you're the
   one managing the page.

Then stop recording and hit submit in the app.

## Do / Don't
✅ **DO** — keep it real, one take, keep moving + talking, show branded gear + a real part, film in daylight.
❌ **DON'T** — don't stage a fake store or office, don't edit or splice clips together, don't read stiffly off
a script, don't film anything you wouldn't want Google to see.

## After
Google reviews it in a **few days**. If it asks you to record again, no big deal — just do the same real walk
one more time. **Text Teddy the moment it says verified** so we can start sending your reviews and photos to
your new page.
