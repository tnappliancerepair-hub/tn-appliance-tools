## Competitor teardown

### Podium — "Answer every lead, even after hours."
- **Why it lands:** every missed call = lost job; after-hours dread.
- **The gap:** their AI takes a message / texts back — doesn't know the caller, can't read the schedule, can't book. A smarter voicemail.
- **Counter:** "Their bot takes a message. Ann books the day."
- **Point at:** call +1 615-588-9400 (greets by name) · demo scene ② "she knows the caller."
- **Ad copy:** *Their AI takes a message. Ours books the job — and knows who's calling before they even say hi. 24/7. Call mine right now: (615) 588-9400.*
- **🔥 Steal:** the after-hours "never miss a lead" fear hook — use their frame, then out-do it (message → booked job).

### Workiz — "You're not married to your software."
- **Why it lands:** owners feel trapped; data hostage.
- **The gap:** they say you're *allowed* to leave; they don't move you. The scary part (migration) is still on you.
- **Counter:** "They say you can leave. We move you."
- **Point at:** import.html wizard · demo Act 3 "bring your data over."
- **Ad copy:** *Switching feels scary because moving your data is scary. So we do it for you — off Housecall Pro, Jobber, or Workiz — in an afternoon, free. And your old system keeps running until you're 100% sure.*
- **🔥 Steal:** the "trapped vs free" emotional frame — exactly the fear our import wizard kills.

### Housecall Pro — "don't tell me what to charge" (from their comments) + per-seat fatigue
- **Why it lands:** the default big name; assumed safe choice.
- **The gap:** per-seat nickel-and-diming; features that feel like being told how to run your shop. The complaints ARE our ad copy.
- **Counter:** "They tell you what to charge. Your customer picks."
- **Point at:** the 4-option pricing (OEM/aftermarket × DIY-ship/we-install) · $99 flat, one login, six seats, no per-seat games.
- **Ad copy:** *We don't tell you what to charge. We show your customer four honest options and let them pick. One price for the whole shop — $99. No per-seat games, no upsell maze.*
- **🔥 Steal:** mine the comment sections under the big-name ads — owners' complaints are pre-written pain points.

### "AI receptionist" ads (FixCall & lookalikes) — a slick spokesperson in a studio
- **Why it lands:** polished, "AI is the future" sheen.
- **The gap:** a spokesperson who's never turned a wrench; thin, phone-only. Answers the phone — then what?
- **Counter:** "You've seen the guy in the suit. I'm the guy in the truck."
- **Point at:** authenticity band · Teddy on camera · the full end-to-end movie (theirs stops at the phone).
- **Ad copy (talking-head open):** *You've seen the AI ads with the guy in the suit. I'm the guy in the truck. I built this because I was drowning in missed calls — and now it runs my whole shop, not just the phone. Here's what it does…*
- **🔥 Steal:** their "try free" CTA + live-proof-card format — swap the actor for YOU.

### Answering services / virtual receptionists
- **Why it lands:** cheap-ish human off-hours coverage.
- **The gap:** a stranger reading a script; no data, no booking, no reschedule; per-minute bill.
- **Counter:** "An answering service reads a script. Ann reads your board."
- **Ad copy:** *An answering service reads a script and takes a message. Ann reads your board — she knows the customer, books the day, moves the appointment, and hands the call to your tech. Same job, done, for a flat rate.*
- **🔥 Steal:** the "never miss a call" promise — then show the actual booking.

## Objection → counter
- **"Scared to switch / lose my data"** → We move it for you and run both side by side until you're sure. Nothing deleted. Zero risk.
- **"Too expensive"** → $99 flat for the whole shop. One missed after-hours call pays for the month.
- **"My guys won't use it"** → Their day is tap-to-navigate and the report fills itself from the call. Less typing than now.
- **"I already have Housecall Pro"** → Keep it. Run us alongside free for 14 days. Not better? Walk away — nothing moved.
- **"Does the AI actually work?"** → Call it right now — (615) 588-9400. It'll know who you are.
