| Metric | Value | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions | 23,309 | Google shows us a lot |
| Clicks | **331** | but few come through |

**Interpretation:** this is exactly the stage a big programmatic build sits at —
lots of indexed pages ranking too *low* (position 30–60) to earn clicks. Not a
failure; a phase. The value they've built is topical authority + internal-link
equity + the occasional long-tail catch.

## 3. Strategy — in order of lead impact

### Tier 1 — Win the terms we already almost rank for (fastest lead upside)
Concentrate authority on the money pages (homepage, Nashville/Antioch/top city
hubs, the top service hubs) for the head terms above.
- Point the whole site's internal links at them with the right anchor text.
- Keep them fresh + deep + conversion-optimized.
- NAP + schema consistency so Google trusts them as the local answer.

### Tier 2 — Win the map pack (biggest UNTAPPED local-lead source)
For "appliance repair near me," the Google 3-pack gets the calls — and it's won
**off the website**, by the Google Business Profile:
- **Kill the "Appliance store / Used appliance store" category** — we still rank
  position 6.4 for "used appliance store near me" = wrong customers. Keep only
  "Appliance repair service" (+ Dryer vent cleaning). *(Teddy off-site action.)*
- **Review velocity** = the #1 map-pack lever. Keep asking same-day; the
  satisfaction-gate + auto-ask are live.
- Photos (crew job photos, automated 2×/wk) + weekly posts (automated) already run.

### Tier 3 — Convert + measure the traffic we already have
- GA4 is on, but only 1 page captures ad clicks and there is **no call tracking**
  — we can't see which pages turn into jobs. Flying blind.
- Add: `tel:` click events, form-submit events, intake-start events → GA4, so we
  KNOW which pages produce work and double down there.
- Every trafficked page: one dominant CTA (call · text-us-back · $50 Quick Check).
  The homepage converts 90% of clicks — protect + sharpen it.

### Tier 4 — The 1,300 long-tail: MAINTAIN, don't expand
- **Keep them indexed.** After the content work they're real pages; they climb
  every day; they cost nothing; a few already convert (oven-repair-hammond,
  washer-repair-new-orleans). Noindexing throws away slow-building upside + the
  internal-link equity that lifts the head terms.
- **Stop building more of them.** Diminishing returns. Effort moves to Tier 1–3.

## 4. The funnel (how a stranger becomes a job)

```
Google search / "near me" / map pack / brand
        │
        ├─ Map pack (GBP) ─────────► phone call 615-280-2949  ► Ann (AI) books it
        │                                                         or human follows up
        ├─ Organic homepage/hub ───► "Book a Repair" / "$50 Quick Check"
        │                                   │
        │                                   ▼
        └─ Long-tail lander ──────► appliance-ai.html (THE primary door)
                                            │  video + model photo + problem
                                            ▼
                                    real tech diagnoses → 4 options → schedule
```
The website's ONE job: get the visitor to call, text, or start the intake. Every
page is judged by that, not by its rank.

## 5. Decisions locked
- Scoreboard = calls/emails/jobs, not indexed-page count.
- Keep all pages indexed; stop scaling the page matrix.
- Next effort goes to: (1) head-term ranking, (2) GBP/map-pack + reviews,
  (3) conversion tracking. In that order.
