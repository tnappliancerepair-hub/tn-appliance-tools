## The core rule you must not break
Google lets you have **one listing per genuine business location.** A second listing is fine — **as long as it's a real, distinct place where you actually operate.** Fake it (PO box, UPS/virtual office, coworking space, a made-up address) and Google **suspends the listing — and can take down your good Antioch one too.** So this only works with a **real Louisiana address.**
