# Power-Page Plan — the pages that dominate for years
_Draft 2026-08-04. Strategy: concentrate authority into a small set of genuinely powerful pages, prune the dead weight, close every page to the simple AI intake in all languages._

## The standard — what every power page must have
- **Clean + readable. One idea per section. Never crowded.** (Teddy's rule.)
- **Real E-E-A-T:** named techs + photos, the real shop video, founder Teddy, "est. 2012," 1,081 reviews.
- **Depth that lasts:** symptoms → likely causes, honest repair-vs-replace, flat-rate labor vs. national all-in pricing, brand coverage, safety flags (gas/240V).
- **Schema:** LocalBusiness + Service + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList (+ Person / VideoObject where relevant).
- **Tools:** model-number finder + part finder widgets.
- **Closing = the simple AI intake** `/appliance-ai.html?appliance=<x>` with a clean **"start in your language"** row (es · vi · fr · ar · hi). No crowding.
- **Tight internal links** to sibling pillars + the right metro hub + related /fix guides.
