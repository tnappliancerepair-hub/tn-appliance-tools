# Tech Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Ant. This is what you need to know in 5 minutes.

## Your tech_id

You'll get a tech_id (a number, like `4`). Save it. Every Ant URL you use needs it.

## Get the PIN from your boss

Your boss texts you a 4-6 digit PIN. Use it the first time you open any Ant page; the page remembers it after that.

## Your URLs (bookmark these on your phone home screen)

- **Daily Dashboard** — `tnapplianceexchange.net/tech-daily-dashboard.html?tech_id=YOUR_ID` — see today's stops + map + sub-nav to other pages.
- **Performance** — see your jobs done, first-visit fix rate, earnings.
- **Payouts** — pending money you're owed.
- **Day Off** — mark a date as off.

The Daily Dashboard has sub-nav buttons (📊 Performance · 💰 Payouts · 🛑 Day Off) so you don't need to remember all the URLs.

## In the truck — Tech Ant Live

When you tap into a job from the Daily Dashboard, Ant Live opens.

**3 things to do per job:**

1. **Tap 🚗 On My Way** when you head out. Customer gets an SMS with your ETA + 1h drive-time-aware ETA.
2. **Narrate findings to the chat.** Talk like you'd tell a buddy:
   > "evap fan motor is dead. compressor runs. swapping it. 1.5h labor."
   Ant listens + suggests parts when relevant.
3. **Tap ✨ Auto-fill TDR from chat.** The TDR form fills itself from your narration. Review + edit + tap Save.

**Then tap Complete (dropdown).** If TDR is incomplete on a warranty job, the system blocks — fix what's missing and try again.

## Quick-action buttons in Tech Ant Live

- **📷 / 🌡 / 💧** — appliance-specific photo prompts (camera opens straight to camera)
- **🔧 Parts** — opens parts-lookup with brand+model pre-filled (4 sources)
- **🧠 Similar** — top-5 historically similar jobs (after the embeddings index has data)
- **✨ Auto-fill from chat** — TDR fields from your chat
