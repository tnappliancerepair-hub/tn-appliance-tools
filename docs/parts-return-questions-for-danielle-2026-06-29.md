# Parts-return system — questions for Danielle (2026-06-29)

Goal: build a return tracker that **protects the techs from surprise chargebacks** —
they know what to send back, by when, and how, with loud reminders, and the shop
only charges a tech who was clearly warned. Danielle owns this process; her answers
set the build. Grouped so she can run through it fast. ⭐ = most important.

## A. The deadline (the heart of it)
1. ⭐ When a part has to go back, where does the **"return by" date** come from — is it
   stated in the return-label email, or is it a fixed number of days from when the
   label is issued?
2. ⭐ Roughly **how many days** do we usually get to return?
3. Is the window the **same for every warranty company** (SquareTrade / AHS / NSA), or
   different per company?

## B. What triggers a return + what goes back
4. Which warranty companies send **prepaid return labels**, and how do they arrive
   (email from `rma_request@squaretrade.com`? a portal? regular mail)?
5. What actually has to go back — the **old/broken part (core)**, **unused/wrong
   parts**, or both? Does it vary by company?
6. Do **Marcone or Amazon** parts ever need to be returned (wrong part, unused, DOA)?
   If so, how does that one work (RMA, different label)?

## C. The physical process (how it actually moves)
7. ⭐ Today, how does a part actually get **shipped back** — the tech drops it at FedEx,
   the office collects + ships, or it's staged somewhere (e.g., "parts @ John's")?
8. Is there a **central spot** returns pile up, or is each tech on his own?
9. Who physically **attaches the label** to the box?

## D. The chargeback mechanics (so we can shield the guys)
10. ⭐ When a part isn't returned in time, how does the warranty company **penalize us** —
    don't pay the claim, charge for the part/core, both? Roughly **how much**?
11. How do we **find out** it happened — an email, a statement, the portal?
12. ⭐ When we then **charge a tech**, how is that decided and how does it come out of his
    pay? **Who decides the amount?**

## E. How we track + close returns today
13. Right now, how do we know a part **was** returned — do we watch FedEx tracking, or
    just trust it got done?
14. Where do you **track open returns** today (the office worklist, a notebook, memory)?
15. Roughly **how many returns are open/owed right now**, and what's the **oldest**?

## F. The fairness policy (the trust piece)
16. Today, do we **always** charge the tech, or only sometimes? What's the current rule?
17. Are you good with the rule: **only charge a tech if the system clearly warned him +
    he still missed it; if we dropped the ball telling him, the shop eats it?**

## G. Volume / where it hurts most
18. Which warranty company is the **worst** for chargebacks / missed returns?
19. About how often is this happening — a few a month, weekly?
