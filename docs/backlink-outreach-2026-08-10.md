## 🥇 TIER 1 — Manufacturer "authorized servicer" directories (highest value)
**Why #1:** these are topically perfect links from domains Google deeply trusts (whirlpool.com, geappliances.com…), *and* getting listed usually means authorized warranty work too. Bigger payoff than 100 of the content pages.

Reality check: some require applying to become a **factory-authorized servicer** (real requirements — insurance, tech certs), not just a listing. Worth it. Start with the ones where you already do the most volume.

|---|---|---|
| **Whirlpool** (Maytag, KitchenAid, Amana, Jenn-Air) | Apply to the Whirlpool service-provider network / get in their "find a pro" locator | whirlpool.com → Owners → Service & Support → schedule/find service; or search "Whirlpool authorized service provider application" |
| **GE Appliances** | GE authorized servicer program + service locator | geappliances.com → Support → Service & Repair; "GE Appliances authorized servicer" |
| **Samsung** | Samsung authorized service center program | samsung.com support → "become an authorized service center" |

| **Electrolux / Frigidaire** | Frigidaire/Electrolux servicer network + locator | frigidaire.com / electroluxappliances.com support |
| **Bosch / Thermador** | Bosch authorized service | bosch-home.com/us support |

- [ ] **Local sponsorships** — Little League team, church, school event, food bank (ties to the Anthony's Gift program) → sponsor pages routinely link to sponsors (a genuine, well-earned local link)

## 🎤 TIER 4 — Founder authority / digital PR (slow but powerful)
You're a *real working technician* — that's a credential most competitors can't fake. Journalists constantly need expert quotes.
- [ ] **Connectively (formerly HARO)**, **Qwoted**, **Help a B2B Writer** — sign up, answer queries like "appliance repair expert: is it worth fixing a 10-year-old fridge?" → earns links from real news/magazine sites (the highest-authority links there are)
- [ ] **Local news** — offer a seasonal expert segment/quote ("dryer fire safety before the holidays," "what to check before you call a repair tech") to Nashville outlets
- [ ] **Your own content earning links** — the /fix/ guides are genuinely good; over time appliance forums (Reddit r/appliancerepair, ApplianceBlog) and DIY sites will link them. Sharing them where relevant (honestly, not spammy) seeds this.

## ⛔ Do NOT
- Don't buy links, "backlink packages," or PBNs — Google penalty, does more harm than good.
- Don't mass-submit to spammy/foreign directories — no value, possible harm.
- Don't vary the NAP — a different phone/address on each listing *cancels* the benefit.
- Don't ever put a personal cell number on a public listing — business lines only (615-280-2949 / 866-268-0111).
