# 🎬 The AssistAnt shop video — script + shot list (Teddy films this)

**What it is:** one 60-90 sec phone video of YOU in your shop showing AssistAnt working. This one clip
becomes the Facebook/Instagram **ad creative**, the **group post**, and the **DM opener** — all three. It's
the single highest-leverage thing we can make, and it's the one thing only you can make: nobody can fake a
real appliance-shop owner talking about the system he built and runs every day. A polished generic ad flops.
You, in your shop, talking straight — that doesn't.

**Golden rule:** real beats slick. Shot on your phone, a little rough, is BETTER here than a produced ad —
it reads as true. Don't over-think it. You can nail this in 20 minutes.

---

## Before you hit record (2 min of prep)
- **Phone horizontal (landscape)** for the main cut (works on Facebook feed + YouTube). We'll also grab a
  **vertical** version for Reels/Stories/TikTok — just re-shoot the same thing turned sideways, or I can
  crop it after.
- **Good light** — face a window or stand where it's bright. Bad light is the one thing that makes it look
  cheap.
- **Quiet-ish spot** — the shop is fine, just not next to a running dryer.
- **Have ready to show on your screen:** the office board (a real day of jobs) and your phone ready to make
  a call to Ann. That's it.
- **Don't script it word-for-word** — glance at the beats below, then say it YOUR way. Stumbling a little is
  fine; it's real.

---

## THE MAIN CUT (60-90 sec) — beat by beat

**BEAT 1 — The hook (first 3 seconds, look right at the camera).** People decide in 3 seconds whether to
keep watching, so open with the pain, not "hey guys."
> *"If you run an appliance shop, you know the phone never stops — and every missed call is a missed job."*

**BEAT 2 — Who you are (5 sec, builds the trust).**
> *"I'm Teddy — I run TN Appliance Exchange. I got tired of missing calls and juggling five different apps,
> so we built our own system. It's called AssistAnt."*

**BEAT 3 — Show Ann answer a real call (the WOW — 20-25 sec).** Call the line, put it on speaker, let the
camera hear Ann greet + start collecting the job. Talk over it lightly:
> *"This is Ann. She answers 24/7 — 2 in the morning, Sunday, doesn't matter. She gets the brand, model,
> what's wrong, whether it's under warranty, and she books it — I don't lift a finger."*
> *(Let a few seconds of Ann actually talking play — that's the proof.)*

**BEAT 4 — Show the board (10-15 sec).** Flip to the office board with a real day on it.
> *"Everything lands right here — one screen, my whole day. No more five tabs. The customer even gets texted
> back automatically and can see where their job stands."*

**BEAT 5 — The two honest edges (10-15 sec).** These are the lines that make a skeptical shop owner lean in:
> *"Two things I'll tell you straight. One — it's a hundred bucks a month, flat, every one of my techs
> included. Not per-seat like the big guys. Two — you can bring your whole book over off Housecall Pro,
> Jobber, or Workiz in an afternoon, and keep your old system running until you're sure. No risk."*

**BEAT 6 — The hive-mind (optional, 8 sec — powerful for the diagnostics angle).**
> *"And here's the part I love: every shop on it feeds one diagnostic brain. The more of us, the smarter it
> gets at 'what usually fixes this' — your jobs make it sharper, and you get everybody else's fixes back."*

**BEAT 7 — The close (5 sec, look at camera).**
> *"If you want to see it, it's at tnapplianceexchange.net/ant — or just message me. I'll show you what it
> does for your shop. Long live the moneymakers."* 🐜

## THE SHORT CUT (30 sec) — for a first ad test / a fast scroll
Hook (Beat 1) → "I built this, it's called AssistAnt" (Beat 2) → 8 sec of Ann answering (Beat 3) → "$100
flat, every tech, bring your data over, keep your old system" (Beat 5) → "see it at tnapplianceexchange.net/ant
or DM me" (Beat 7). No board, no hive-mind — just the hook, the wow, the offer, the ask.

## Shot list (grab these clips, we assemble after)
1. **You, talking to camera** — the hook + who you are + the close (Beats 1, 2, 5, 7). Do it in one take if you
   can; if not, one clip per beat is fine.
2. **Ann answering a call** — phone on speaker, screen or just audio, 20-30 sec of her actually working a job.
3. **The office board** — a real day, scroll it a little so it's clearly live.
4. *(nice to have)* **B-roll:** your van, a tech at a customer's door, a repair in progress — 3-5 sec each,
   silent, to cut over the talking. Makes it feel like a real shop (it is).
5. **A vertical (phone-upright) version** of clips 1 + 2 for Reels/Stories/TikTok.

---

## Do / Don't
- ✅ DO look at the camera on the hook + the close. DO let Ann actually talk (the proof is her voice). DO say
  it your way. DO keep it under 90 sec — shorter is better.
- ✅ DO make only true claims (they're all true above): 24/7 answering, $100 flat/all techs, bring-your-data +
  keep-old-system, the shared diagnostic brain, a real shop that built it.
- ❌ DON'T script it stiff or read it off a page monotone. DON'T over-produce it. DON'T claim it replaces a
  master tech or that it's "the biggest network" (it's growing — say "gets smarter as shops join," which is
  true). DON'T put your personal cell in it — the CTA is the site + DM.
