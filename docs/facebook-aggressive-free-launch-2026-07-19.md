## THE FREE LEVERS — all maxed
1. **Daily posting (Ant-automated).** Min 1 post/day on the page, every day — archive drip + AI-demo + proven-format reruns. Consistency is what the algorithm rewards.
2. **Personal page = the #1 free multiplier.** Teddy posts the reintroduction on his *personal* page (personal reach ≫ page reach), friends + family share. Their shares are the real engine — turns a 6K page into a 60K reach.
3. **The "movement" share-ask.** Not "please share my business" — *"help me put a family shop back on the map."* People share missions, not ads.

5. **Reply to EVERY comment, fast (Ant).** Engagement velocity boosts reach. Ant drafts/handles replies at scale.
6. **Cross-post everything.** One clip → FB + IG + YouTube Shorts + TikTok. Free reach on four surfaces.
7. **Go Live weekly.** FB Live gets boosted reach — a "watch this repair" or a fix-it Q&A.
8. **Local group participation (Teddy, genuine).** Answer real appliance questions in Nashville/Murfreesboro/Smyrna community groups. ⛔ **NO spam / auto-DM / scraping** (instant ban + off-brand) — only Teddy participating like a helpful neighbor.

10. **Nostalgia reactivation.** "12 years ago today…", tag old customers, "where are they now."
11. **Stories + Reels daily.** The surfaces FB pushes hardest right now.
