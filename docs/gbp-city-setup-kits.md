## The 6 non-negotiable rules (these keep you off Google's tripwires)
1. **Register as a Service-Area Business — address HIDDEN.** When asked "do customers come to this address?"
   → **No.** (Correct for repair + hides the tech's home publicly, but keeps it verifiable.)
2. **Exact real name on every profile: `TN Appliance Exchange`.** Never add the city ("…Clarksville" =
   keyword-stuffing = fast flag). City ranking comes from the service area + reviews, never the name.
3. **A distinct local number per profile** (below). Never reuse the Antioch number — duplicate phone is the #1
   thing that makes Google merge/suspend profiles as duplicates.
4. **A real, non-overlapping service area per profile.**
5. **The tech records the video verification himself** at his home — van, tools, parts, branded gear. Honest
   because it's real; it's exactly what passes Google's video check now.
6. **Get the tech's written OK** to use his home as the registered (hidden) address + do the verification.
