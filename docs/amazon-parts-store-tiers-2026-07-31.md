# TN Appliance Parts Store — the price-leader ladder (2026-07-31)

**Teddy's vision (2026-07-31):** be the store that has EVERY part at EVERY tier, each at the
best price — cheapest open-box/return parts, name-brand OEM at best prices, ERP + Supco
off-brand at best prices, dryer-vent parts at best prices, hoses at best prices, and
everything in between. Sell it on Amazon (+ our own site).

**Why it's a moat, not just a price race:** nobody offers *all tiers of the same part* in one
trusted, tech-run store. A buyer picks their budget (open-box → off-brand → OEM) and gets our
fitment + AI "is this right?" on every one. We compete on price AND win on trust.

## The engine that makes "best prices" real
**Cheapest-source price engine** — for any part number, query every source we have (own
open-box inventory → CoreCentric reman → ERP/Supco/TJ90 off-brand → Marcone OEM), take the
lowest landed cost, apply the margin rule, publish a competitive price, reprice continuously.
It's an extension of what already exists (`marcone-lookup` + `parts-autoplace`). This is the
backbone that delivers "best prices" without hand-pricing thousands of SKUs.

## Honest tension (surfaced, not ignored)
Being cheapest on *every single SKU* vs Encompass/PartSelect/OEMs is a margin knife-fight.
What makes "best prices" **survivable for us specifically**:
- **The extra tier they don't have** — open-box/reman. We can be genuinely cheapest there.
- **Low returns** (fitment + AI confirm) — on Amazon low returns protect RANK, not just margin.
- **Our own external traffic** (449 pages + base) → Brand Referral Bonus (~10% back) + rank.
So: price-competitive across the ladder, differentiated by the tiers + trust + AI.
