## STEP 2 — Register at sell.amazon.com
- Choose the **Professional** selling plan ($39.99/mo) — required for the Buy Box, categories,
  and bulk listing. (Individual plan can't win the Buy Box — skip it.)
- Enter business info + EIN, complete the **tax interview** (W-9), verify identity + bank.
- Amazon may request a business doc (e.g., utility bill / bank statement) — have one ready.

## STEP 3 — Fulfillment: launch FBM (recommended)
- **FBM (Fulfilled by Merchant)** = we ship / drop-ship from Marcone. **Zero upfront inventory,
  zero capital.** Uses the drop-ship spine we already have.
- Move proven sellers to **FBA** later (Prime badge + returns-resell) once volume justifies stock.

## STEP 4 — Start the trademark (slow, parallel — start NOW)
- File a trademark on "TN Appliance Exchange" (or a product-line brand). Takes months.
- Unlocks **Brand Registry** → branded Store page, A+ content, Sponsored Brand ads, hijack
  protection — and is the path to the **Amazon Renewed** (open-box tier) application.
