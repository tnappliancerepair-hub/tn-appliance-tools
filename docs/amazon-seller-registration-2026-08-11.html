✅ The order of operations

  1. Go to sell.amazon.com → Sign up → choose the Professional plan.
  2. Sign in with the existing tnappliance@gmail Amazon login (keeps buyer + seller under one roof).
  3. Enter Business information (copy the fields above) → business type = privately-owned.
  4. Add the beneficial owner (you) → residential address + DOB.
  5. Enter the billing card.
  6. Do the Tax interview (the W-9 answers above).
  7. Add the deposit bank account.
  8. Identity verification — upload ID + a document, do the quick photo/video check.
  9. Account goes "under review" → live once verified (usually a few days).
