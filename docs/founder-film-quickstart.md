## Do it today — 5 steps
1. **Film your bits (~15 min, phone is fine).** Shoot S3, S5, S6 to camera (kit §3 has the exact lines — say
   them plainly, one take each, look at the lens) + a quick S1 shot (pre-dawn, in the van, phone lit with
   missed calls). Shoot **vertical 9:16**, good daylight or a warm work light, phone braced/steady.
2. **Screen-record S4 (~30 sec).** Record the real board doing its thing — a call answering, an appointment
   landing, a part flagged, a route drawing, a customer text going out. Use the **demo tenant** so there's no
   real customer data on screen. → _I can stage a clean demo-board screen-rec walkthrough for you to capture._
3. **Generate the B-roll you didn't film.** Pick ONE AI video tool and paste the kit §3 prompts for S1/S2:
   - **Sora** (OpenAI) or **Google Veo 3** — best realism, easiest. **Runway Gen-4** or **Kling** — also great.
   - One 8-sec clip per prompt. Keep them moody/cinematic; they're just texture behind the VO.
4. **Voiceover.** Two options:
   - **Best: read it yourself** for the founder film (S1/S2/S4 narration + your to-camera lines are already you).
     Record in a quiet room, phone voice-memo is fine.
   - **Or ElevenLabs TTS** — the moment you vault `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` (admin-secrets.html), the connector's
     live and _I render an mp3 per line from the kit script_ (settings: stability 0.5 / similarity 0.75 /
     style 0.3). Also unlocks a **Spanish dub** of the finished spot for the TN market.
5. **Assemble + post.** Drop the VO under the picture in **CapCut** (or hand the clips + VO to an editor),
   add captions + a soft bed of music, end on the sign-off plate (🐜 AssistAnt · $99/mo flat · built by a real
   shop · tnapplianceexchange.net/ant). Export **9:16 master** (+ 1:1 and 16:9 if easy). Then upload the
   finished clip in **`video-studio.html`** → tap **Post everywhere** → it fans to FB / IG Reel / TikTok /
   YouTube with per-platform copy. Done.
