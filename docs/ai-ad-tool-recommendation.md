# 🎬 "Put yourself in an AI-generated ad" — which tool, and why (for Teddy)

_You saw a **Render AI** ad ("put yourself in an AI-generated ad, concept to finished ad in minutes"). This
is the straight answer on which tool to use, tuned to the one thing that makes your ads win: **you're a real
appliance-shop owner.** Companion to `docs/founder-film-quickstart.md` + `docs/commercial-production-kit.md`._

---

## The verdict (one line)
**Film the real founder ad yourself** (no tool needed for your face — the script's already written), and buy
**HeyGen ($29/mo, free tier lets you test with 3 videos)** as the "make-an-ad-in-minutes" tool to clone
**your own** face for the fast social cuts, the consumer spot, and a Spanish version. **Never use a
stock-actor tool for anything "owner/founder."**

## Why the real-face rule matters (your moat)
Your edge over FixCall AI and HighLevel is simple: **a real shop owner built it and runs it.** That's the one
thing they physically can't say.

- A tool that puts a **stock AI actor** on screen playing "a shop owner" is a lie — it throws away the exact
  edge you win on. Skip those for founder/owner ads.
- A tool that clones **your own face**, saying **your own words**, about **your own product**, disclosed as
  an ad — that's honest. It's your likeness, your message.
- But a clone is still synthetic. So for the **anchor founder film** — the one you send warranty companies,
  creators, and drop in the appliance groups — genuinely-you on camera beats a clone. Save the clone for
  speed and volume.

**My pick: HeyGen.** One subscription clones you from a ~15-second video, then generates as many ads as you
want in your real likeness, with sharp identity + micro-expressions, in **175 languages** (so you get a
Spanish version for the TN market from the same tool). Start on its **free tier** (3 videos) before you pay a
dime. Captions is the solid #2 if you'd rather work 100% from your phone (it clones from one selfie).

**Arcads/Creatify are great tools — for the wrong job.** They shine when you want to A/B-test dozens of UGC
hooks with actors. That's a *different, later* play for the SaaS, and it's **never** the move for "I'm a real
shop owner who got tired of losing." Don't let one narrate your story.

## How to run it (both ads, founder first)
1. **Founder anchor — film it real.** Phone, navy work shirt, warm workshop light, one take per line. The
   full 6-scene script + shot list is in `docs/founder-film-quickstart.md` and `commercial-production-kit.md
   §3`. Screen-record the real AssistAnt board for the product beat. Polish captions in Submagic/CapCut.
2. **Everything else — HeyGen clone.** Record your one ~15-sec clone clip, then generate: the fast SaaS cuts,
   the **"Broken at 2am? We Answer"** consumer spot (script in `commercial-production-kit.md §4`), and a
   Spanish version. Real-your-likeness, minutes each.
3. **Distribute — already built.** Drop the finished clip in `video-studio.html` → tap **Post everywhere** →
   it fans to FB / IG / TikTok / YouTube with per-platform copy. Same one tap for every version.

## The honesty line (keep it)
Real-you for the anchor. Your-own-clone (never a stock actor) for volume and dubs. Every claim true. Always
disclosed as an ad. Don't let a tool put words in a fake person's mouth about your shop.
