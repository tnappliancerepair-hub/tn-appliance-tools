## 3) Targeting (who to show it to)

- **Location:** start US-wide, or narrow to your strongest metros first (TN + LA) to keep CPM low.
- **Age:** 28–60. **Gender:** all.
- **Detailed targeting — interests/employers (layer these in "narrow audience"):**
  - Housecall Pro, Jobber, Workiz, ServiceTitan, FieldEdge (people who follow competitor pages)
  - Field service management, Small business software
  - Appliance repair, HVAC, Plumbing, Handyman, Electrician
  - Small business owners, Self-employed
- **Behaviors:** Small business owners, Facebook Page admins, Business page admins.
- **Custom Audiences (highest ROI — set these up):**
  - Upload your **contact list** (other shop owners you know, trade contacts) → target them directly.
  - **Website visitors** (needs the Pixel) → retarget everyone who hit `home.html` but didn't sign up.
  - **Lookalike (1–3%)** off your signups once you have ~20–50 — Meta finds more owners like them.
