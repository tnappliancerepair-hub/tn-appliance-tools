About our text messages

TN Appliance Exchange LLC sends transactional SMS only — appointment confirmations, technician arrival times, parts updates, diagnostic results, and a quick check-in after your service. We never send marketing or promotional messages.

Examples of what we'd text you:

  • "TN Appliance update: Your technician [name] is on the way and will arrive in approximately [eta] minutes. Reply STOP to opt out."
