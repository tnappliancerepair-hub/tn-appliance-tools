DROP YOUR LOGOS HERE
====================

Two files. Keep these exact filenames so the site picks them up automatically.

1. melissa-wood-logo.png
   - Your personal agent logo / headshot mark / monogram
   - Recommended: 200x200px (square), transparent PNG
   - Shows in the site header next to your name
   - If you don't have a personal logo yet, use your professional headshot
     cropped to a square

2. florida-executive-realty-logo.png
   - The official Florida Executive Realty brokerage logo
   - Recommended: 600x240px (wide), transparent PNG
   - Shows in the footer for compliance attribution
   - Get the latest version from your broker's marketing portal — they
     usually have official brand-approved versions in a downloadable kit

UNTIL YOU DROP THESE FILES IN:
The site will silently hide the logo images (they have onerror handlers)
and fall back to the text-only "Melissa Wood / Florida Executive Realty"
branding that's already in place.

ONCE YOU DROP THEM IN:
The header gets your personal logo next to the brand text, and the footer
shows the official FER brokerage logo for legal compliance.

FLORIDA LAW REMINDER:
Florida real estate law requires the brokerage name be displayed
prominently on all advertising. Both text ("Florida Executive Realty") AND
the logo presence in the footer satisfies this requirement. Don't remove
either.
