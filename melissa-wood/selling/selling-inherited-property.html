Probate and step-up basis

What you'll learn

This guide covers everything Tampa Bay sellers need to know about probate and step-up basis — from the first conversation to the closing table. Whether you're new to the area or you've been here for decades, the Tampa Bay market has unique factors that make working with a local Realtor critical.

The Tampa Bay context

Probate and step-up basis in Tampa Bay specifically means understanding factors that don't apply in other markets: hurricane insurance requirements, flood zones (AE vs X), wind mitigation credits, HOA structures, retirement-friendly tax considerations, and seasonal market patterns.
