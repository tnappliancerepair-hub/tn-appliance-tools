Field Academy

The method, the meter, and the deep guides — for the crew. Study it, then keep it in your pocket on the job. This is internal — built from how we diagnose.

The standard: first-visit fix. Confirm with the meter — don't guess. Continuity + voltage finds ~95% of everything. Give the customer honest options. Beat the machine: the more you nail the exact part before the truck rolls, the faster the day goes and the bigger the check.

1 · The 95% method — power → switch → load

Almost every fault is one broken link in a simple circuit. Find the broken link and you've found the repair.

1 · Power source

Outlet, cord, plug, breaker. 120V most, 240V for dryers/ranges/ovens (lose a leg = runs but no heat).

2 · Switch / control

Door/lid switch, thermostat, thermal fuse, timer, relay, pressure switch, board — the gatekeepers. Pass power only when conditions are right.

3 · Load

Element, motor, pump, valve coil, compressor, igniter — the part doing the work. Loads almost always fail open.

Test order: (1) power arriving? (voltage) → (2) switch passing it? (continuity) → (3) load good? (continuity). Whichever fails is the repair. Full write-up: how to diagnose any appliance →

Jacks: black → COM; red → VΩ for volts/ohms/continuity; red → 10A only for an amp test (fused). Verify first: touch probes together on continuity — should beep / ~0Ω.

⚠ Ohms/continuity = power OFF + isolate the part (disconnect a lead) or a sneak path fools you. Voltage = power ON, careful. Never ohm a live circuit. Microwave HV cap can kill even unplugged — leave it.

3 · Reading cheat-sheet

ReadingMeans
~0 Ω / beepGood, unbroken path.
OL / infiniteOpen — broken path or dead load. Usually your bad part.
120V / 240V presentPower arriving as expected.
0V where you expect itPower not getting there — look upstream.

Values vary by model — you're asking "in the ballpark, or wide open?" Open is the clear tell almost every time.

5 · Brand quirks & fault codes

Same physics, brand-specific tells. A few worth knowing cold:

• Samsung fridge shows OF OF = store DEMO mode, won't cool — exit demo first. RF French-door = evap frost-up (defrost sensor/heater).
• LG fridge — 2014–2017 linear compressors had an extended-warranty wave; check coverage before condemning.
• Whirlpool/Maytag washer F9 E1 = long drain (pump/hose/filter); F5 = lid lock.
• Bosch dishwasher E24/E25 = drain (filter/pump/check-valve; base-pan float stops drain).
• LG OE = drain error (washer + dishwasher). Samsung 5C/SC/nd = drain.
• Dryer blocked vent blows the thermal fuse — replace the fuse AND clear the vent or it blows again.
