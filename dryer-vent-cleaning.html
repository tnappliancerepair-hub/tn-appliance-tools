🎖️ CSIA Certified — C-DET, the real credential

Dryer vent work isn't regulated, so anybody with a shop-vac calls themselves a "vent cleaner." We hold the C-DET — Certified Dryer Exhaust Technician — the credential issued by the CSIA (Chimney Safety Institute of America), the national body that certifies dryer-exhaust and chimney pros. It means we're trained to NFPA/IRC code on airflow, duct routing, and the fire-hazard points most people never check. When you're picturing who to call for your dryer vent, this is who you want on the roof. We are the guys for the job.
