FAQ

Abita Springs Customers Ask

Why does my ice maker freeze up or quit in the summer?
Heat and humidity stress the fill valve and water line. Mineral deposits from hard water buildup clog the inlet, or the valve itself freezes closed. We clean or replace the valve and flush the line—usually a half-day job that keeps ice flowing year-round.
How often should I have my AC serviced in Abita Springs?
Once a year before June is standard; many homeowners do it twice—spring and fall—to catch refrigerant leaks early and keep the condenser coil clear of pollen and humidity crud. A clean coil means your compressor isn't gasping.

Is It Worth Fixing?

Our Philosophy in Abita Springs

A refrigerator that's been humming reliably for twelve years deserves a shot at repair if the compressor is still sound and a condenser cleaning might buy you another cycle. But if the sealed system is failing or the repair costs climb above half a new unit's price, we'll tell you straight—replace it, get a warranty, and move on. Same logic with AC: a freon top-up and a new contactor might solve it today; a failing compressor means a new unit is the honest path. We fix what makes sense and retire what doesn't.

Try the replacement calculator for a quick framing, but every situation is different. The Technician Decision Report gives you the real numbers on your specific machine — not a generic age formula.
