Diagnose Your Problem

Common Freezer Symptoms

Whatever your freezer is doing, it points at a specific part. Here's how the common symptoms map to the real cause — the diagnosis nails which one it is on your exact model.

Freezer Not Freezing (Running, But Warm)

Compressor hums but nothing gets cold: usually the defrost system, a stuck evaporator fan, or a refrigerant/sealed-system issue. A chest freezer that runs warm is often a failed start relay or a slow leak.

Heavy Frost / Ice Buildup

Thick frost on the walls or coils is a classic defrost failure (heater, defrost thermostat, or timer/control) or a bad door seal letting warm, humid air in. The frost eventually blocks airflow and the freezer stops cooling.

Freezer Running Constantly / Never Shuts Off

A freezer that never cycles off is fighting a bad thermostat/cold control, dirty condenser coils, a worn door gasket, or low refrigerant. It spikes your power bill and burns out the compressor if ignored.

Too Cold / Freezing Everything Solid

Over-freezing or frozen-food-burn points at a stuck thermostat or a failing control board that won't cut the compressor at the set point.

Water on the Floor / Leaking

Puddles usually mean a clogged defrost drain (frost melts and overflows) or, on frost-free uprights, a cracked drain pan. Easy to misdiagnose — a tech confirms it fast.

Loud Buzzing, Clicking, or Humming

Clicking every few minutes with no cooling is often the start relay cycling on a compressor that won't start. Loud buzzing can be the evaporator or condenser fan hitting ice or a failing motor.

Is It Worth Fixing?

Repair vs. Replace a Freezer

Freezers are some of the most repair-worthy appliances in the house. A chest or upright freezer is a simple, durable machine, and the common failures — defrost parts, relays, thermostats, fans — are cheap relative to a new unit (and a new one means losing your food while you shop). A sealed-system/compressor repair is the one to think hard about on a small unit; on a large or built-in freezer it's usually still worth it. The replacement calculator gives you a quick framing, and our technicians give you the real numbers on your specific machine.
