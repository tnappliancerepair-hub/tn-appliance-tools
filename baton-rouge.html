Why does my refrigerator keep losing temperature even though it sounds normal?
In Baton Rouge's humidity, the most common culprits are a clogged condenser coil (dust and moisture build up fast here), a failing compressor, or a worn door seal letting warm air leak in. Sometimes it's the defrost drain backing up. A tech can check all of these in one visit and tell you which one is actually causing the problem.
Should I replace my AC unit when it's over 10 years old?
Not automatically. If it's cooling, not running constantly, and repair costs stay reasonable, it can keep going. But if you're replacing the compressor, or it's cycling on and off all day in this heat, you're probably near the end. A real diagnosis beats an age-based guess.

Is It Worth Fixing?

Our Philosophy in Baton Rouge

Repairing an appliance makes sense when the fix is straightforward and the unit still has years of reliable life ahead. In Baton Rouge, where temperatures and humidity are relentless, a well-repaired refrigerator or freezer can run solidly for years more if the real issue—a bad compressor, a clogged line, a worn seal—is caught early. Replacing a 5- or 6-year-old appliance just because one part failed usually isn't the right call, especially if you've taken care of it. But if a unit is 12+ years old, failing often, or the repair costs more than half the price of a new one, a technician will say so clearly. We won't push you either way.
