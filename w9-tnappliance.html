Your W-9 is filled in — just sign it

Set up as a single-member LLC you report on your personal return (what "we filed together, it's personal" means): Individual / sole proprietor is checked, your name is on Line 1, the LLC name on Line 2, EIN in Part I. All you have left is:
  1. Sign & date (Part II) — that's it.
  2. Confirm Line 1 matches your 1040 — it should read your legal name exactly as it appears on your personal return. Fix the spelling if it's off.

One "what if": if your accountant says the LLC files its own partnership return (Form 1065 — possible since you and your wife own it together), instead check the LLC box with code P and put TN Appliance Exchange LLC back on Line 1. One change, then sign.
