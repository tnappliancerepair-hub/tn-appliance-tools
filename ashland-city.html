FAQ

Ashland City Customers Ask

Why does my water heater keep failing, even though it's only a few years old?
Well water carries sediment and minerals that settle inside the tank and clog the heating element. Your heater works harder to reach temperature, corrodes faster, and quits sooner. A flush and anode-rod check can add years; if you haven't had those done, that's often the first move.
Should I switch to city water to save on appliance repairs?
That's between you and your utility options. But even on city water, mineral content varies. Installing a whole-house sediment or softening system upstream is often cheaper and easier than watching each appliance suffer separately.
