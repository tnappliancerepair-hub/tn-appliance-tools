What does the E15 error code mean on my Bosch dishwasher?

E15 on a Bosch dishwasher means water collected in the base pan and tripped the Aquastop leak-protection float — the dishwasher shut itself off to prevent a flood, and it often runs the drain pump nonstop until the base is dry. It's a smart safety feature, and sometimes it clears with a simple reset, but it's usually telling you water got somewhere it shouldn't. Here's what a tech does.

⚠ Safety first: Low-risk to try the reset, but E15 means water has leaked into the base — if it keeps returning, there's an active leak that needs finding before it causes floor or cabinet damage. Turn off the water supply if it's leaking freely.

The most likely causes

Water pooled in the base pan (reset) Moderate DIY check

A one-time overfill, a heavy suds event, or a small splash can trip the float. Often the fix is just drying the base so the float drops back down.

Check it: Tip the dishwasher back gently (or prop the front up) to drain water out of the base pan, let it dry, and set it upright. Then run a short cycle — if E15 is gone and doesn't return, it was a one-time trip.

Detergent / rinse-aid overdose (excess suds) Easy DIY check

Too much detergent or the wrong kind creates suds that overflow into the base and trip the float.

Check it: Use the correct dishwasher detergent and amount; run a rinse cycle to clear existing suds after drying the base.

An actual leak (hose, pump seal, tub) Moderate DIY check

If E15 comes right back after drying the base, water is actively leaking in — from a hose connection, the pump seal, the spray arm supply, or a tub crack.

Check it: This is the tech diagnosis — finding and fixing the leak source is what clears E15 for good.

Stuck float switch Moderate DIY check

The Aquastop float itself can stick up (from debris) and report water that isn't there.

Check it: After drying the base, make sure the small foam float in the base moves freely; a stuck float is a tech fix.

Is it worth fixing?

Repair — a reset is free, and even a leak fix (a hose, a seal) is far below the cost of a new dishwasher. The important thing is not to ignore a returning E15, since it means water is getting loose.

Common questions

How do I reset the E15 code on my Bosch dishwasher?

Tip the dishwasher back to drain water out of the base pan and let it dry so the Aquastop float drops, then set it upright and run a short cycle. If E15 is gone, it was a one-time trip. If it returns, there's an active leak to find.

Why does my Bosch dishwasher keep showing E15?

A returning E15 means water is actively leaking into the base — a hose, pump seal, spray-arm supply, or tub. That needs a tech to locate and fix the leak; drying the base only resets it temporarily.
