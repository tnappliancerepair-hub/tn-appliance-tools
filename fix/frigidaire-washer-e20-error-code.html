What does the E20 (or E21) error code mean on my Frigidaire washer?

E20 (some models show E21) on a Frigidaire front-load washer is a drain error — the machine tried to pump the water out and it didn't leave fast enough, so it stops with water in the tub. The most common cause is something you can clear yourself in a few minutes. Here's the order a tech works it.

⚠ Safety first: Low-risk. The drain filter holds water — have a towel and shallow pan ready before opening it. Unplug the washer if you pull it out to reach the drain hose.

The most likely causes

Clogged drain pump filter Easy DIY check

The #1 cause. Lint, coins, and small items collect in the pump filter behind a small panel on the lower front and block the drain.

Check it: Open the lower-front panel, lay down towels, unthread the round filter slowly (water will come out), and clean everything out. Run a rinse+spin to confirm E20 is gone.

Kinked or clogged drain hose Easy DIY check

A drain hose kinked behind the washer, or shoved too far down the standpipe, blocks the water and triggers E20.

Check it: Pull the washer out gently, straighten any kink, and make sure the hose only goes a few inches into the standpipe.

House drain / standpipe clog Moderate DIY check

If the wall standpipe is partially clogged, water backs up and the washer reads a failed drain.

Check it: Remove the hose from the standpipe and pour water in — if it backs up, the house drain needs clearing.

Failed drain pump Technician

If the filter and hoses are clear but E20 persists, the drain pump has likely failed.

Check it: Listen for the pump during the drain step — no sound with a clean filter points at the pump, a straightforward tech replacement.

Is it worth fixing?

Almost always repair — most E20 codes clear for free by cleaning the pump filter, and even a failed drain pump is an affordable part plus flat labor.

Common questions

How do I fix the E20 code on my Frigidaire washer?

Clean the drain pump filter behind the small panel on the lower front (towels ready — water comes out), then check the drain hose for kinks. That clears the large majority of E20/E21 codes.

Where is the drain filter on a Frigidaire washer?

Behind a small access panel on the lower-front of the machine. Open it and you'll find a round threaded filter — that's it. It holds a fair amount of water, so drain it slowly into a pan.

How much does it cost to fix a Frigidaire E20 code?
