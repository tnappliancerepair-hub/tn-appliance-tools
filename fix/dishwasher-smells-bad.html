My dishwasher smells bad — what do I do?

A dishwasher that smells — musty, eggy, or like old food — almost always has food debris or standing water sitting somewhere it shouldn't. The good news: nearly every cause is something you can clean yourself, for free. Here's the order a tech works it.

⚠ Safety first: All low-risk cleaning steps — no tools or power work needed.

The most likely causes

Clogged filter (the #1 cause) Easy DIY check

Food and grease collect in the filter at the bottom of the tub. A dirty filter is the most common source of a dishwasher odor.

Check it: Remove the bottom rack, twist out the cylindrical filter at the tub floor, and wash it in hot soapy water. Wipe out the sump area underneath before putting it back. Do this monthly.

Trapped food in the spray arms, edges, or door Easy DIY check

Bits of food lodge in the spray-arm holes, along the tub edges, and in the door gasket, and rot over time.

Check it: Clear the spray-arm holes with a toothpick, wipe the tub edges and the rubber door gasket/seal, and clean around the detergent door.

Dirty drain hose / standing water Easy DIY check

A partly-clogged filter or drain hose leaves water standing in the bottom that goes stagnant and smells; grease film in the hose adds to it.

Check it: Run an empty hot cycle with a cup of white vinegar on the top rack (and/or a dishwasher cleaner) to flush grease and freshen it. If water always stands in the bottom, see our "won't drain" guide.

Drain hose routed wrong (sewer smell) Moderate DIY check

A true sewer smell can mean the drain hose isn't looped high enough (needs a high loop under the sink) or the air gap is missing, letting sink-drain odor back up.

Check it: If it smells like sewer (not food), a tech or plumber checks that the drain hose has a proper high loop / air gap.

Is it worth fixing?

Almost always a free cleaning fix — the filter, spray arms, gasket, and a vinegar cycle solve the vast majority of odors. Only a drain-routing or standing-water issue needs a closer look.

Common questions

Why does my dishwasher smell bad?

Almost always trapped food — most often in the filter at the bottom of the tub, plus the spray arms, tub edges, and door gasket. Clean the filter, wipe the gasket, and run a hot vinegar cycle. That clears most odors.

How do I clean a smelly dishwasher?

Pull and wash the bottom filter in hot soapy water, clear the spray-arm holes, wipe the door gasket and tub edges, then run an empty hot cycle with a cup of white vinegar on the top rack. Do the filter monthly to keep it fresh.

Why does my dishwasher smell like sewage?

A sewer smell (vs food) usually means the drain hose isn't looped high enough under the sink or the air gap is missing, letting drain odor back up. That's a quick routing fix a tech or plumber handles.
