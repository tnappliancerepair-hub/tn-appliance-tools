My LG refrigerator ice maker stopped working — what do I do?

LG refrigerators make great ice when everything's flowing, so when the icemaker stops it's usually because water, temperature, or the assembly itself has a problem. Here are the real causes in the order a tech checks them, plus the safe things to try yourself first.

⚠ Safety first: Low-risk checks only — unplug the fridge before reseating the icemaker or bin.

The most likely causes

Water filter overdue Easy DIY check

A clogged LG filter chokes the water supply to the icemaker — the most common reason production slows or stops. LG recommends replacing it about every 6 months.

Check it: Replace the filter, dispense water several times to purge air, and allow 24 hours for a batch.

Freezer not cold enough Easy DIY check

LG icemakers need the freezer around 0°F to harvest ice on schedule. A warm freezer — often from blocked vents or a weak seal — cuts production sharply.

Check it: Set the freezer to 0°F, keep food clear of the vents, and check the door seal.

Kinked, disconnected, or frozen water line Moderate DIY check

The supply line behind the fridge can kink, work loose, or freeze, starving the icemaker. Low home water pressure can do it too.

Check it: Check that the line behind the fridge isn't pinched and the saddle/shutoff valve is fully open. A frozen line clears with a 24-hour power-off defrost.

Icemaker needs a reset Easy DIY check

LG icemakers have a Test/reset button; a stalled harvest cycle can sometimes be kicked back to life without any parts.

Check it: Press and hold the Test button on the icemaker until it chimes and cycles — keep hands clear as the tray rotates. If it won't complete a cycle, it needs a tech.

Failed icemaker assembly or inlet valve Moderate DIY check

When water, temp, and resets are all good and there's still no ice, the icemaker assembly or the water inlet valve has failed and needs replacing.

Check it: This is the tech diagnosis and repair — we confirm the failed part and replace it.

Is it worth fixing?

Repair — LG icemaker assemblies and inlet valves are affordable and the fridge is worth keeping. Replacement only comes up on an old unit with a sealed-system failure on top of the ice issue.

Common questions

How do I reset my LG ice maker?

Press and hold the Test/reset button on the icemaker until it chimes and starts a cycle (keep hands clear of the rotating tray). If it won't complete a cycle, check the filter and freezer temp, then call a tech.

Why is my LG refrigerator not making ice?

Most often an overdue water filter, a freezer running too warm, or a kinked/frozen water line. Change the filter, confirm the freezer is at 0°F, and check the line behind the fridge before assuming the icemaker failed.
