My oven door is locked and won't unlock — what do I do?

An oven door that won't unlock is almost always the self-clean lock that didn't release — the oven locks the door during a self-clean cycle and only unlocks once it has cooled below a safe temperature. Most of the time it just needs to finish cooling or get a reset. Here's how to get it open safely, and what it means if it stays stuck.

⚠ Safety first: Do not force the door — the lock is metal and forcing it can bend the latch or damage the door. If a self-clean cycle was running, the oven can still be very hot inside; give it time to cool before expecting it to release.

The most likely causes

Still cooling from self-clean (most common) Easy DIY check

The oven keeps the door locked until the interior drops below a safe temperature after self-clean — that can take 30–90 minutes. It's working as designed.

Check it: Wait until the oven is fully cool. Many models won't show "unlock" or release the latch until then. Once cool, the lock should release on its own or when you cancel the cycle.

Control glitch — needs a reset Easy DIY check

The lock motor cycles on a control signal; a glitch can leave it stuck in the locked position even after the oven is cool.

Check it: With the oven cool, unplug the range (or flip its breaker) for 5 minutes, then restore power. The reset often re-runs the lock cycle and releases the door.

Failed door lock motor / assembly Technician

The real mechanical cause. The self-clean lock has a small motor and switches; when it jams or fails, the latch stays engaged and often throws a lock-related code.

Check it: If the oven is cool and a reset didn't free it, the lock motor/assembly is the likely fix — a tech replaces it.

Lock switch or control board Technician

A failed lock switch (which tells the control the door's position) or the control board itself can leave the oven thinking it should stay locked.

Check it: A tech tests the lock switch and control after ruling out the motor.

Is it worth fixing?

Usually repair — most stuck locks are just cooling or clear with a reset (free). If the lock motor/assembly failed, it's an affordable common part with flat labor, well below a new range.

Common questions

How do I unlock my oven door after self-clean?

First, let it fully cool — the oven keeps the door locked until the inside drops below a safe temperature, which can take up to 90 minutes. If it's cool and still locked, unplug the range (or flip the breaker) for 5 minutes to reset it; that usually re-runs the lock cycle and releases the door. Never force it.

Why is my oven door still locked when it's cool?

If it's fully cooled and a power reset didn't release it, the self-clean lock motor or its switch has likely failed and is holding the latch engaged — an affordable, common repair a tech handles.

Can I force an oven door open when it's locked?

No — forcing it can bend the latch or damage the door and won't fix the cause. Let it cool, try a reset, and if it stays locked have the lock assembly checked.
