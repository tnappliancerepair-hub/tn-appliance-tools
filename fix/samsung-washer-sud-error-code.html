What does the Sud (or SUDS/5D) code mean on my Samsung washer?

Sud (also shown as SUDS or 5D) on a Samsung washer means the machine detected too many suds and paused to let them settle — it can't spin safely through a tub full of foam. The good news: this is one of the most often free-to-fix codes there is. Here's the order a tech runs through it.

⚠ Safety first: All low-risk checks — no tools or power work needed.

The most likely causes

Too much detergent, or non-HE detergent Easy DIY check

By far the #1 cause. High-efficiency washers use very little water, so a normal dose — or any non-HE detergent — creates way more suds than the machine can handle.

Check it: Use only HE detergent and cut the amount (often just 1–2 teaspoons for a normal load). Run an extra rinse to clear the current suds. This alone fixes most Sud codes.

Suds not clearing (needs a rinse/spin) Easy DIY check

Once suds are present, the machine keeps reading Sud until they're gone — it can look "stuck" on the code.

Check it: Run a rinse + spin (or a Self Clean / tub-clean cycle with no detergent) to flush the foam out, then try your load again.

Slow drain letting suds linger Moderate DIY check

If water + suds drain slowly, foam sits in the tub longer and trips Sud. A partly-clogged debris filter is the usual culprit.

Check it: Clean the debris filter behind the small door on the lower-front (towels ready — water comes out) so the machine can flush suds out fully.

Is it worth fixing?

Almost always a free fix — it's usually a detergent habit, not a broken part. If Sud keeps coming even with a tiny amount of HE detergent and a clean filter, a tech can check the drain and sensor.

Common questions

How do I get rid of the Sud code on my Samsung washer?

Run a rinse + spin (no detergent) to clear the foam, then going forward use only HE detergent and much less of it — often just 1–2 teaspoons. Too much detergent is the cause the vast majority of the time.

Why does my Samsung washer keep saying Suds?

Either you're still using too much or non-HE detergent, or suds aren't draining out (a partly-clogged debris filter). Cut the detergent way down and clean the filter behind the lower-front door.

Is the Sud code a serious problem?

Usually not — it's the washer protecting itself from over-sudsing, and it's almost always a free detergent fix. If it persists with correct detergent and a clean filter, your $50 Quick Check credits toward any repair.
