What does the C1 code (or standing water) mean on my GE dishwasher?

C1 on a GE dishwasher (you'll also just see water standing in the bottom) means the machine isn't draining in the time it expects. Most drain problems trace to something you can clear yourself. Here's the order a tech works it, cheapest first.

⚠ Safety first: Low-risk. If you pull the dishwasher out to reach the drain hose or power, shut off its breaker first.

The most likely causes

Clogged filter / standing debris Easy DIY check

The most common cause. Food, glass bits, and grease collect in the filter at the bottom of the tub and block the drain path.

Check it: Remove the bottom rack, twist out the cylindrical filter at the tub floor, rinse it clean, and clear any debris in the sump underneath before reseating it.

Blocked or new air gap / disposer plug Easy DIY check

If the drain routes through a sink air gap or a garbage disposal, a clogged air gap — or a disposal that was just installed with its knockout plug still in — stops the dishwasher from draining.

Check it: Clean the air gap on the sink (pop the cap, clear it). If a disposal was recently installed, confirm the knockout plug was removed.

Kinked or clogged drain hose Moderate DIY check

A drain hose kinked behind the dishwasher or where it connects under the sink restricts the flow.

Check it: Check the hose under the sink and behind the unit for kinks or clogs and straighten/clear them.

Failed drain pump Technician

If the filter, air gap, and hose are all clear but water still stands, the drain pump has likely failed.

Check it: If everything upstream is clean and it still won't drain, this is the tech diagnosis and pump replacement.

Is it worth fixing?

Almost always repair — most C1 codes clear for free by cleaning the filter or air gap, and even a drain pump is an affordable part plus flat labor.

Common questions

How do I fix the C1 code on my GE dishwasher?

Clean the filter at the bottom of the tub and the sink air gap first — those clear most C1 / standing-water cases. Then check the drain hose for kinks. If it still won't drain, the pump needs a tech.

Why is there standing water in my GE dishwasher?

It's not draining — usually a clogged filter or air gap, a kinked drain hose, or (if a garbage disposal was just installed) the disposal knockout plug left in. Clear those before suspecting the pump.

How much does it cost to fix a GE dishwasher that won't drain?
