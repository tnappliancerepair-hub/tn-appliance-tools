My washer won't turn on at all — what do I do?

A washer that's completely dead — no lights, no response — usually has a simple, findable cause, and several you can rule out in a minute before calling anyone. Here's the order a tech checks it, starting with the free stuff.

⚠ Safety first: Low-risk checks. If you pull the washer out to reach the outlet, unplug it first; if it's hardwired or on a GFCI, don't poke at wiring — check the breaker instead.

The most likely causes

No power at the outlet / tripped breaker Easy DIY check

The most common cause of a totally dead washer: a tripped breaker, a GFCI outlet that popped, or a plug that worked loose (common with vibration).

Check it: Check the breaker (flip it fully OFF then ON), press RESET on any GFCI outlet, and make sure the plug is firmly seated. Test the outlet with another device if you can.

Control lock / child lock is on Easy DIY check

Many washers have a control-lock button that disables the panel, so buttons do nothing. Easy to switch on by accident.

Check it: Look for a Lock or child-lock button (often a padlock icon) and hold it a few seconds to turn it off.

Lid/door not latched Easy DIY check

A washer often won't start (and may look unresponsive) if it doesn't sense the lid or door fully closed and latched.

Check it: Close the lid/door firmly until it clicks, and make sure nothing is blocking it.

Failed lid switch, door lock, or control Technician

A failed lid switch/door lock, a bad power cord, or the main control board can leave the washer dead even with good power.

Check it: If power is confirmed good, nothing's locked, and the lid latches but it's still dead, this is the tech diagnosis and repair.

Is it worth fixing?

Usually repair — most dead-washer cases are free (breaker, GFCI, plug, lock, latch), and a lid switch or control is an affordable part well below a new washer.

Common questions

Why won't my washer turn on at all?

Most often it's a power issue — a tripped breaker, a popped GFCI outlet, or a loose plug — or the control lock is on. Check those first: reset the breaker/GFCI, seat the plug, and turn off any child-lock.

How do I reset a washer that won't turn on?

Unplug it (or flip its breaker off) for one minute, then restore power — that clears many control glitches. Then confirm the lid/door latches and the control lock is off.
