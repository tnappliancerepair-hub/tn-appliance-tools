What does the HE (or HC) error code mean on my Samsung dryer?

HE on a Samsung dryer is a heating error — the dryer's temperature sensors saw something out of range and shut the heat down to stay safe. It can be as simple as a restricted vent choking the airflow, or a heating component that's failing. Because it involves heat and airflow, start with the vent (which is also the fire-safety issue) and go from there.

⚠ Safety first: ⚠️ A heating error often traces back to a restricted vent, which is a fire hazard. Clean the lint trap and check the vent before running more loads. Any element/thermostat replacement should be done by a tech with the power off.

The most likely causes

Restricted exhaust vent (check first) Moderate DIY check

A clogged vent traps heat, spikes the internal temperature, and trips the HE safety code — and it's the leading cause of dryer fires, so it matters most.

Check it: Clean the lint trap, pull the dryer out and check the duct behind it, and check the exterior vent flap. A full vent-run cleaning clears the restriction and the fire risk — that's our dryer-vent cleaning service.

Failed heating element Moderate DIY check

If airflow is good but the dryer overheats or the element shorts, HE can appear. The element is the part that actually makes the heat.

Check it: If the vent is clean and clear but HE persists, the heating element or its circuit needs a tech to ohm it out and replace.

Faulty thermistor (temp sensor) Moderate DIY check

The thermistor reports drum temperature to the control. If it reads wrong, the control sees a false high/low and throws a heating error.

Check it: A tech ohms the thermistor against spec — an affordable sensor replacement if it's out of range.

Thermostat / thermal fuse Moderate DIY check

Safety thermostats and the thermal fuse can fail (often after a vent restriction cooked them), cutting or misreading heat.

Check it: These are inexpensive parts a tech replaces — and they should be checked alongside clearing the vent that likely stressed them.

Is it worth fixing?

Repair — start with a vent cleaning (often the whole fix, and a safety must), and the heat-side parts (element, thermistor, thermostat) are all affordable relative to a new dryer.

Common questions

What does HE mean on a Samsung dryer?

It's a heating / high-temperature error — the dryer shut the heat off because a temperature sensor read out of range. The most common root cause is a restricted vent; less often it's the heating element or thermistor.

How do I fix the HE code on my Samsung dryer?

Clean the lint trap and clear the exhaust vent first — a restricted vent is the top cause and a fire risk. If HE stays after the vent is clean, a tech checks the heating element, thermistor, and thermostats.

Is a Samsung dryer HE code dangerous?

It's a safety shutdown, so the dryer protected itself — but the common cause (a clogged vent) is a genuine fire hazard. Clear the vent promptly. Your $50 Quick Check credits toward any repair if a part is needed.
