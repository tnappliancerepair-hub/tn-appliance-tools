My refrigerator water dispenser stopped working — what do I do?

When the water dispenser in your fridge door slows to a trickle or stops, it's almost always about water getting TO the door — a frozen line, a clogged filter, or a supply problem. Most of these you can check yourself. Here's the order a tech runs through them.

⚠ Safety first: All low-risk checks — no tools or power work needed.

The most likely causes

Frozen water line in the door Easy DIY check

The thin water tube running up into the door can freeze if the freezer is set too cold or the line has a slow spot — the most common reason a dispenser suddenly quits while ice still works.

Check it: Set the freezer no colder than 0°F and stop dispensing for a few hours, or leave it off overnight, to let the line thaw. If water returns, the line was frozen.

Water filter overdue or clogged Easy DIY check

A filter past its ~6-month life chokes water flow to both the dispenser and the icemaker. A brand-new filter with trapped air can also sputter at first.

Check it: Replace the filter, then dispense several cups to purge the air. If unsure, briefly remove the filter and dispense — if flow returns, the filter was the problem.

Low household water pressure Easy DIY check

Dispensers need roughly 40 psi to work well. A partly-closed supply valve or low home pressure gives a weak trickle.

Check it: Check that the saddle/shutoff valve behind or under the fridge is fully open and the line isn't kinked.

Control lock / dispenser lock on Easy DIY check

Many fridges have a dispenser lock button that disables the pad, easily switched on by accident.

Check it: Look for a Lock button on the dispenser panel and hold it to turn it off.

Failed water inlet valve or dispenser switch Moderate DIY check

The inlet valve that lets water in, or the dispenser switch/actuator behind the pad, can fail so nothing comes out even with good pressure and a fresh filter.

Check it: If the line isn't frozen, the filter's new, pressure is good, and it's unlocked but still nothing, this is the tech diagnosis and repair.

Is it worth fixing?

Repair — most causes are free (thaw the line, unlock) or a cheap filter; an inlet valve or dispenser switch is an affordable part. Replacing the fridge over a dispenser almost never makes sense.

Common questions

Why is my fridge water dispenser not working but ice still works?

That points to a frozen water line in the door or an overdue filter — the dispenser line freezes or clogs while the icemaker keeps going on its own supply. Thaw the line and change the filter first.

How do I unfreeze my refrigerator water line?

Set the freezer to 0°F (not colder) and stop using the dispenser for several hours, or turn the fridge off overnight, to let the door line thaw. If water returns afterward, freezing was the cause — a tech can add insulation or fix the cold spot if it keeps happening.
