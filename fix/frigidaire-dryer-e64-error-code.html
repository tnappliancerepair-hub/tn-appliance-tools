What does the E64 error code mean on my Frigidaire dryer (no heat)?

E64 on a Frigidaire dryer means the control detected an open circuit in the heating element — an electrical break in the part that makes the heat. The drum still tumbles, but clothes come out cold or barely warm. It's a specific enough code that a tech usually knows where to look. Here's how it's worked.

⚠ Safety first: ⚠️ Electric dryers run on 240V. Checking the vent is fine, but ohming or replacing the heating element is done by a tech with the dryer unplugged — the element circuit carries serious current.

The most likely causes

Failed (open) heating element Moderate DIY check

The most common cause of E64 by far. The heating element coil breaks over time, opening the circuit so no heat is produced — the control reads that open and reports E64.

Check it: A tech ohms the element; an open reading confirms it. It's a common, affordable part replacement that restores heat.

Broken wiring or connector to the element Technician

A burned or disconnected wire/terminal feeding the element can look like an open element and trigger E64 even if the element is fine.

Check it: A tech inspects the element's wiring and connectors for burns or a loose terminal while testing.

Thermal fuse or limiter in the heat circuit Technician

A blown thermal fuse or open thermal limiter in series with the element also opens the heat circuit — often after a vent restriction overheated it.

Check it: A tech checks the thermal fuse/limiter alongside the element, and will clear the vent that likely caused it (so it doesn't blow again).

Is it worth fixing?

Repair — a heating element (or a thermal fuse) is a common, affordable part plus flat labor, far below a new dryer. E64 is very fixable.

Common questions

Why does my Frigidaire dryer run but not heat with an E64 code?

E64 means the heating-element circuit is open — the element (or a fuse/wire in its circuit) has an electrical break, so the drum tumbles but no heat is made. The heating element is the usual culprit.

How much does it cost to fix a Frigidaire E64 code?

Usually an affordable heating element (or a cheap thermal fuse) plus flat labor. Your $50 Quick Check credits toward the repair.

Should I clean my vent if I get an E64 code?

Yes — a clogged vent overheats the heat circuit and can blow the fuse or element that caused E64, so clearing the vent is part of a proper fix so it doesn't happen again. Ask about our dryer-vent cleaning.
