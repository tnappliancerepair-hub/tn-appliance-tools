What does the UE error code mean on my LG washer?

UE on an LG washer means the machine detected an unbalanced load and stopped the spin to protect itself — it doesn't want to bang the drum around off-center. A lot of the time this is a quick fix you can do yourself, but if it keeps coming back on normal loads, something mechanical is wearing out. Here's the order a tech checks it.

⚠ Safety first: Low-risk checks. Unplug the washer before pulling it out to check leveling or dampers.

The most likely causes

Load bunched to one side Easy DIY check

The most common cause: a few heavy items (towels, a comforter, jeans) clump on one side, so the drum can't balance for the high-speed spin.

Check it: Open the door, redistribute the load evenly around the drum, and restart the spin. Add a towel or two to balance a single bulky item like a comforter.

Washer not level Easy DIY check

If the machine isn't sitting level, it rocks and reads as unbalanced even with a normal load. Common after a move or on an uneven floor.

Check it: Check with a level on top; adjust the leveling feet until it's solid and doesn't rock when you push a corner. Tighten the lock nuts.

Overloaded or single-item loads Easy DIY check

Too big a load, or washing one heavy item alone, both make balancing impossible and trip UE.

Check it: Split large loads; pair a single bulky item with a few smaller ones.

Worn shock absorbers / dampers or suspension Moderate DIY check

If UE keeps happening on normal, well-distributed loads and the washer is level, the shock absorbers or suspension that steady the drum are worn out — the drum swings too far and trips the sensor.

Check it: This is the tech diagnosis and repair — worn dampers are an affordable part plus labor and restore normal spin.

Is it worth fixing?

Repair — most UE codes are free (redistribute, level), and worn dampers/shocks are an affordable part well below the cost of a new washer.

Common questions

How do I fix the UE code on my LG washer?

Open the door and redistribute the load evenly, make sure the washer is level and not rocking, and avoid washing one heavy item alone. That clears most UE codes. If it keeps returning on normal loads, the shock absorbers are likely worn.

Why does my LG washer keep saying UE every load?

If UE happens on well-distributed, level loads, the drum's shock absorbers or suspension are worn and letting it swing too far — a tech replacement. Persistent UE is the sign it's mechanical, not just the load.
