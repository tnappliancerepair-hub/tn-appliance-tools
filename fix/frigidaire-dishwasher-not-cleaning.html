Frigidaire Dishwasher Not Cleaning?

A Frigidaire dishwasher that won't clean is most often a clogged filter or spray arm, or water that isn't hot enough to dissolve detergent. Frigidaire dishwashers are straightforward — the cheap, checkable causes fix the large majority of "it stopped cleaning" cases.
The known Frigidaire issue: Frigidaire dishwashers depend a lot on genuinely hot incoming water. If yours cleans poorly, run the kitchen hot tap until it runs hot BEFORE starting the cycle — a surprising number of "won't clean" Frigidaires are just being fed cold water at the start.

What's actually different about a Frigidaire

Clogged filter and sump Easy

The bottom filter and sump pack with food and grease so dirty water recirculates.
What you can check: Twist out and rinse the filter under hot water, clear the sump, and run it monthly. Run the sink's hot tap before starting.

Blocked spray arms Easy

Clogged jets or a stuck arm leave the top rack dirty.
What you can check: Pull the spray arms, clear each jet with a toothpick, and confirm they spin freely.

Water not hot enough Moderate

Frigidaire relies heavily on incoming hot water plus a heater; a cold fill or failed heater leaves grease and detergent behind.
What you can check: Run the kitchen hot tap until it's hot, then start the dishwasher. If the tub is cold at cycle-end, the heater circuit needs a tech.

Weak inlet valve / wash pump Pro

Low fill (inlet valve) or low spray pressure (wash pump) both leave dishes dirty after the cheap causes are ruled out.
What you can check: A technician checks the inlet valve and wash pump last.
Try this first: Cancel/drain by pressing the Cancel button (dishes may need to be restarted), then cut power at the breaker for a minute to clear the control.

How this failure works on any dishwasher

A dishwasher that runs a full cycle but leaves food, film, or grit behind is rarely broken — most of the time it's starved of one of the three things it needs to clean: a clear filter, freely spinning spray arms, and hot water. Most of these you can fix yourself in a few minutes. Here's the order a tech checks them.

Safety first: These checks are low-risk, but pull the door open mid-cycle carefully — the water inside is very hot.

Clogged filter Easy

Most modern dishwashers have a removable filter in the bottom that traps food. When it packs with gunk, dirty water just recirculates onto your dishes. This is the single most common cause of poor cleaning.
What you can check: Twist out the cylindrical filter in the floor of the tub, rinse it under the tap, and scrub it with a soft brush. Do this monthly and cleaning improves dramatically.

Blocked spray arms Easy

The spray arms have small jets that can clog with hard-water scale, seeds, and debris. If a jet is blocked or an arm can't spin freely, whole sections of the rack never get sprayed.
What you can check: Pop off the spray arms (usually a twist or clip), poke each jet clear with a toothpick, and make sure they spin freely by hand. Also check that tall items aren't blocking them.

Water isn't hot enough Easy

Dishwashers need about 120°F water to dissolve detergent and cut grease. If the incoming water is cold — a common winter problem — detergent won't activate and dishes come out filmy.
What you can check: Run the kitchen sink hot for a few seconds before starting the dishwasher so hot water reaches it right away, and use the "heated" or "sanitize" option.

Hard-water buildup / no rinse aid Easy

Mineral scale coats the tub, jets, and heating element over time, and skipping rinse aid leaves spots and film. Both look like "not cleaning" even when the machine is mechanically fine.
What you can check: Keep the rinse-aid dispenser filled and run a dishwasher cleaner or a cup of vinegar through an empty hot cycle monthly.

Worn wash pump or chopper Moderate

The wash pump pressurizes the spray arms and a chopper grinds food fine enough to rinse away. When either wears out, spray pressure drops and cleaning suffers even with a clean filter.
What you can check: If the filter, arms, and water temp are all good and dishes are still dirty, this is the tech diagnosis — a pump or chopper repair.

Is it worth repairing?

Almost always repair — the top causes are free or a cheap part, and even a wash-pump job is well under the cost of a new dishwasher plus installation. Replacement only makes sense on a very old unit facing a pump failure plus other issues.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my Frigidaire dishwasher not cleaning?

Is there a known issue with Frigidaire dishwashers not cleaning?

Frigidaire dishwashers depend a lot on genuinely hot incoming water. If yours cleans poorly, run the kitchen hot tap until it runs hot BEFORE starting the cycle — a surprising number of "won't clean" Frigidaires are just being fed cold water at the start.

Why is my dishwasher not cleaning the top rack?

The top rack is usually fed by the middle spray arm — if its jets are clogged or it can't spin, or the top rack's water supply tube is blocked, the top comes out dirty while the bottom is fine. Clear the arms and make sure nothing blocks them.

Why are my dishes gritty or filmy after washing?

Gritty means a clogged filter recirculating food; filmy usually means water that's not hot enough, no rinse aid, or hard-water scale. Clean the filter, pre-run hot water at the sink, and keep rinse aid filled.
