What does the F21 error code mean on my Whirlpool washer?

F21 on a Whirlpool front-loader (you'll also see it as "F21" or a long drain warning) means the washer couldn't pump the water out in the time it allows, so it stops with a load sitting in water. The usual culprit is a clogged pump filter — the "coin trap" — and clearing it is something most people can do themselves. Here's the exact order a tech works it.

⚠ Safety first: Low-risk, but the coin trap holds a lot of water — have a shallow pan and several towels ready before you open it. Unplug the washer if you pull it out to reach the drain hose.

The most likely causes

Clogged drain-pump filter / coin trap Easy DIY check

This is the overwhelming cause of F21. Coins, lint, hairpins, and small items collect in the filter behind the small panel on the lower front and block the drain.

Check it: Open the lower-front access panel, put down towels, and slowly unthread the round filter — a lot of water will come out, so drain it in stages into a pan. Clean everything out and reseat it snugly.

Kinked or clogged drain hose Easy DIY check

A drain hose kinked behind the washer, or pushed too far down the standpipe, stops the water from leaving and triggers F21.

Check it: Pull the washer out gently, straighten any kinks, and make sure the hose only goes a few inches into the standpipe.

House drain / standpipe clog Moderate DIY check

If the wall drain the hose empties into is partially blocked, water backs up and the washer reads it as a failed drain.

Check it: Remove the hose from the standpipe and pour water in — if it backs up, the house drain needs clearing.

Failed drain pump Moderate DIY check

If the coin trap and hoses are clear but F21 persists, the drain pump motor has likely failed.

Check it: Listen during the drain step — no pump sound with a clean filter points at the pump, a straightforward tech replacement.

Is it worth fixing?

Almost always repair — most F21 codes clear for free by cleaning the coin trap, and even a failed drain pump is an affordable part plus flat labor, nowhere near a new washer.

Common questions

How do I fix the F21 code on my Whirlpool washer?

Clean the drain-pump filter (coin trap) behind the small panel on the lower front — put down towels, drain the water out slowly, and clear everything caught inside. Then check the drain hose for kinks. That fixes the large majority of F21 codes.

Where is the coin trap on a Whirlpool washer?

Behind a small access panel on the lower-front of the washer. Pop the panel, and you'll see a round threaded filter — that's it. Have a pan and towels ready; it holds a surprising amount of water.

How much does it cost to fix a Whirlpool F21 code?
