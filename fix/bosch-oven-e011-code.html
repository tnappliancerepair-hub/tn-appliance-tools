What does the E011 code mean on my Bosch oven?

E011 on a Bosch oven is a door-lock fault — the oven tried to lock or unlock the door (usually around a self-clean cycle) and the lock didn't reach its position, so the control flags it and often leaves the door stuck. Most of the time it clears once the oven has cooled and been reset. Here's how to get it open safely, and what it means if it stays.

⚠ Safety first: Do not force the door — the lock is metal and forcing it can bend the latch. If a self-clean cycle was running the interior can still be very hot; let it cool fully before expecting the lock to release. Any panel work should be done with the range unplugged.

The most likely causes

Still hot / needs to finish cooling Easy DIY check

The oven keeps the door locked until the inside cools below a safe temperature after self-clean, and it can flag E011 if it checks the lock before that.

Check it: Let the oven cool completely (up to 60–90 minutes after self-clean). Many times the lock releases and E011 clears on its own once it's cool.

Control glitch — reset it Easy DIY check

The lock motor runs on a control signal; a glitch can leave it stuck mid-travel with E011 showing even after it's cool.

Check it: With the oven cool, unplug the range (or flip its breaker) for 5 minutes, then restore power. The reset re-runs the lock cycle and often frees the door and clears E011.

Door lock motor / assembly failed Technician

The real mechanical cause. The lock has a small motor and switches; when it jams or fails it can't reach the locked/unlocked position, so the control throws E011.

Check it: If the oven is cool and a reset didn't clear it, the lock motor/assembly is the likely fix — a tech replaces it.

Lock switch or wiring Technician

A failed position switch (which tells the control the lock reached its spot) or a loose connector reads like a lock that never got there.

Check it: A tech tests the lock switch and its wiring after ruling out the motor.

Is it worth fixing?

Usually repair — most E011s are just cooling or clear with a reset (free). If the lock motor/assembly failed, it's an affordable common part with flat labor, well below a new oven.

Common questions

How do I fix the E011 code on my Bosch oven?

First let it cool completely — after a self-clean the oven won't release the door until it's below a safe temperature, and E011 often clears then. If it's cool and still showing E011, unplug the range (or flip the breaker) for 5 minutes to reset it; that re-runs the lock cycle and usually frees the door. If it stays, the door lock motor/assembly has failed and gets replaced. Never force the door.

Why is my Bosch oven door locked with an E011 code?

E011 means the lock didn't reach its position. If it's fully cool and a reset didn't release it, the self-clean lock motor or its switch has failed and is holding the latch — an affordable, common repair.
