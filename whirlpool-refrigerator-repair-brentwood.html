Typical Whirlpool refrigerator failures we confirm first

  • Defrost system — heater, bi-metal thermostat, or the adaptive defrost control — causing the classic "freezer cold, fridge warm."
  • Evaporator fan motor gone noisy or dead, so cold air never reaches the fresh-food side.
  • Icemaker module or water inlet valve — no ice or water pooling under the drawer.
