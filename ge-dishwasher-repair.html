Common Causes

What Usually Goes Wrong on GE Dishwashers

💧 Won't fill with water (we got ya)

No water coming in is almost always the water inlet valve, a stuck float / float switch, or GE's flood-float safety that has tripped — sometimes a supply-line or a control telling the valve not to open. Easy to pin down and affordable to fix. We confirm the exact cause so you don't replace the wrong part.

💀 Completely dead — won't turn on

No lights, buttons do nothing? A dead GE dishwasher is usually a blown thermal fuse, a failed door latch/switch, or the main control board — and sometimes just power at the outlet. All common, fixable failures. Easily brought back to life, all GE models.

Not draining

Standing water in the bottom is usually the drain pump — clogged with debris or worn out — or a blocked drain hose. A fast, affordable repair.

Not cleaning / not drying

Gritty dishes point to a worn wash pump, clogged spray arms, or a failing inlet valve under-filling the tub; won't-dry is usually the heating element or vent. We diagnose which one instead of guessing.

Control board & buttons

Dead buttons, stuck cycles, or error codes can be the main control. We confirm the board is actually the fault — via SmartHQ on newer units — before replacing it.
