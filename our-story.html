Where it started

Our story starts back in the 1970s, in Florida, when a young Jimmy Pivacek started junking old machines for gas money — and realized he could fix them himself. His brother Billy started flipping them, and the two of them lived their best free-roaming lives, chasing the work wherever it took them. Along the way they taught John and a young Teddy how to make it happen.

Teddy swore he'd do anything but appliances. Instead he got cursed to a lifetime of it — and somewhere in all those years, he fell in love with getting better at it. There was plenty of failure. There were several used-appliance shops. And eventually Teddy was teaching his own brother and his own sons the same trade — cursing them to the same lifetime.

In 2012 that heritage became TN Appliance Exchange. We embraced the curse and made it our destiny: to be the best.

When one of us pulls up to your door, you already know who it is — we text you first. Every tech is licensed, insured, and background-checked — that's how we earned Google's Google Guaranteed badge and over 1,000 reviews at 4.5 stars.
