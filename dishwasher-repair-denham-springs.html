Common Dishwasher problems we see in Denham Springs, LA

Around Denham Springs — the Antique Village and the neighborhoods off I-12, here are the dishwasher calls that come in most — and what they usually turn out to be:

  • Won't drain / standing water — a clogged filter or drain hose, or a failed drain pump — the filter is the one almost nobody cleans.
  • Dishes come out dirty — a clogged spray arm, a worn wash pump, or hard-water scale caked on the jets.
  • Leaking — a torn door gasket, a loose hose clamp, or a cracked sump.
  • Won't start or no power — the door-latch switch, a blown thermal fuse, or the control board.
  • Not drying — a failed heating element, or an empty rinse-aid reservoir.
  • Smells — the filter and sump just need cleaning — usually not a failure at all.
