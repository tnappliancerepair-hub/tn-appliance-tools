Common Dryer problems we see in Laplace, LA

Around LaPlace — the Airline Hwy corridor in St. John the Baptist Parish, here are the dryer calls that come in most — and what they usually turn out to be:

  • No heat, clothes still damp — most often a clogged vent choking the airflow — but it can be a blown thermal fuse, a failed heating element, or (on gas) a worn igniter.
  • Takes two cycles to dry — almost always restricted venting or a lint-packed blower wheel — the dryer is fine, the airflow isn't.
  • Won't start — usually a door switch, a blown thermal fuse, or the start switch — sometimes just a tripped breaker on the 240-volt circuit.
  • Won't tumble but hums — a snapped drive belt or a worn idler pulley.
  • Squealing or thumping — worn drum rollers, the idler pulley, or a failing rear drum bearing.
  • Shuts off early — a failing moisture sensor, or a cycling thermostat reading a false “dry” from poor airflow.

Dryers are one of the most repair-worthy appliances you own — most fixes are a small fraction of a new unit, and the machine usually outlives the part that failed. We'll only wave you off if the drum is rusted through or the cabinet's cracked. Gas models and the 240-volt circuit are a call-a-pro job — we handle those safely.
